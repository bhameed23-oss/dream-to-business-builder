# Local AI Helper Setup

This module helps users explore local or private AI helper setups for business use.

## Concept

Some teams want to use AI or local language models for internal business operations without sending everything to a cloud service. Others prefer a secure, private, or self-hosted setup.

## Considerations

- what data will the model access?
- is the data sensitive?
- do you need human approval before actions happen?
- are there local hardware or software requirements?
- is there a fallback if the model is unavailable?

## Best practice

Keep the AI system narrow, transparent, and reviewed. It should support the workflow, not replace business reasoning or human accountability.
