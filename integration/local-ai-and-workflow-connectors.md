#Local AI and Workflow Connectors

This file outlines how local AI helpers and workflow connectors can support the business lifecycle without creating chaos.

## Purpose

This system should help a business use AI in a clear, controlled, and useful way.

## Good use cases

- summarizing notes or customer intake information
- drafting follow-up messages
- helping organize internal business notes
- reviewing workflow steps for clarity
- helping the user understand operations or missing steps
- supporting staff with internal process summaries

## Poor use cases

- full automation with no human oversight in sensitive workflows
- sending customer data to tools without review
- making decisions on customer trust or safety without human approval
- using AI in a way that hides important reasoning from the operator

## Safety guardrails

- keep the AI helper narrow and specific
- avoid sending unnecessary sensitive data
- require human review prior to risky actions
- keep a clear record of prompts and outputs when relevant
- keep the tool explainable and measurable

## Why it matters

AI becomes useful when it reduces friction and complexity, not when it adds confusion or unnecessary risk.
