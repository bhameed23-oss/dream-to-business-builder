# Smart Automation Patterns

Automation should support people, not create confusion.

## Good automation patterns

- appointment reminders
- lead follow-up
- contact enrichment
- internal status updates
- document or form collection
- recurring reporting
- workflow status notifications
- reviewer alerts

## Poor automation patterns

- bringing together sensitive data without review
- auto-sending customer messages without oversight
- triggering actions without human review in sensitive cases
- automating decisions without clear logic

## Design principle

Automation should make a business easier to operate, not harder to trust.
