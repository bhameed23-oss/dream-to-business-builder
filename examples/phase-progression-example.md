# Phase Progression Example

This is a sample progression for a local service business.

## Example

A local service business begins in Creation with:
- a clear offer
- a target customer
- a basic customer journey
- risk review for customer data and payment handling

Then it moves into Operation where:
- bookings, follow-up, and scheduling are handled by the founder
- manual follow-up leads to missed touchpoints
- customer quality and communications vary

Then it enters Auditing and identifies:
- poor intake consistency
- missing records
- poor follow-up quality
- no clear risk review or compliance boundaries

Then Scaling shows that the business is not ready for more work because:
- the founder is still the bottleneck
- scheduling is too fragile
- customer communications vary

Then Compliance Catch-Up reveals:
- customer data and contact records need more structure
- marketing consent needs review
- payment and document handling may need safeguards

Then Automation & Systems Upgrade reduces chaos by:
- standardizing reminders
- improving intake flow
- centralizing records
- creating better status updates

Then Exit Readiness asks:
- is the business transferable?
- does it depend too much on the founder?
- can someone else run it?
- what would a buyer need to see?

## Why this matters

This example makes it clear that business progression is not random. It is layered, interconnected, and should be guided intentionally.
