# Example Business Flow: Service Business

This is a sample lifecycle flow for a local service business.

## Example scenario

A founder wants to start a local cleaning business.

### Creation
- define the service and price model
- identify customer type and market area
- map the initial operation process
- review privacy and business risk
- set a minimal launch plan

### Operation
- handle booking and customer contact
- manage service scheduling
- send reminders
- create follow-up communications
- monitor service quality and repeat customers

### Auditing
- review whether customer intake is consistent
- review what is done manually
- review whether operational quality is slipping
- review whether there are missing risk reviews

### Scaling
- prepare staffing or subcontractor systems
- create repeatable quality standards
- improve scheduling and communication
- review whether payment and customer records are consistent

### Compliance Catch-Up
- review customer contact storage
- review data handling and consent for marketing
- review whether policies and records are adequate

### Automation & Systems Upgrade
- automate reminders and follow-up
- centralize customer records
- standardize scheduling
- route tasks and service updates

### Exit Readiness
- document the process
- improve process dependency on founder knowledge
- clean up customer data and records
- assess whether the business is transferable or saleable
