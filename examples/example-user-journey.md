# Example User Journey

This is an example of how a new user could move through the project.

## Example scenario

A person wants to start a cleaning business but does not know where to begin.

### Step 1: Discovery
The system asks:
- What kind of business do you want to build?
- Who do you want to serve?
- What jobs do you do?
- How do customers book currently?
- What problems do you want to solve?

### Step 2: Assessment
The system identifies:
- a local service business model
- likely repeated tasks and recurring customers
- manual scheduling and follow-up needs
- customer communication needs
- basic privacy and data handling concerns

### Step 3: Risk and compliance
The system asks:
- Do you collect customer contact details?
- Do you handle payments?
- Will you store customer records?
- Do you need a privacy policy or terms of service?

### Step 4: Design and journey
The system suggests:
- simple brand message
- clear booking flow
- service pages
- follow-up messaging
- trust-building site language

### Step 5: Operations and automation
The system suggests:
- booking reminders
- estimate follow-up
- customer satisfaction review requests
- staff scheduling support
- CRM or simple contact tracking

### Step 6: Launch readiness
The system reviews:
- website quality
- security basics
- consent and messaging clarity
- launch checklist
- quality control expectations

### Step 7: Improvement
The system supports ongoing review and refinement as the business grows.
