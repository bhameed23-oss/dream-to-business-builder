# Example Business Flow: Auto Body or Vehicle Service Business

This is a sample lifecycle flow for a vehicle repair or vehicle service business.

## Example scenario

A founder wants to build an auto body or repair-focused business.

### Creation
- define the offer and target customer type
- create the intake process for repairs
- identify the minimum workflow needed for customer trust
- review data and repair record handling requirements

### Operation
- process repair intake and estimate workflow
- coordinate repair status updates
- manage customer communication and reminders
- ensure records are consistent and reviewable

### Auditing
- identify where communication breaks down
- review if repair status updates are missing or delayed
- review if customer files are incomplete or scattered
- review access to records and sensitive information

### Scaling
- standardize repair intake and status workflows
- improve staff coordination and communication flow
- review how tickets, customer records, and approvals are managed

### Compliance Catch-Up
- review customer and vehicle document handling
- review insurance communication and data privacy practices
- review access control and record retention

### Automation & Systems Upgrade
- automate status update messages
- create estimate follow-up flows
- standardize repair progress updates
- connect digital intake and repair records

### Exit Readiness
- document the intake, repair, and customer communication process
- reduce founder dependency in operational decisions
- clean up records and workflow ownership
- assess whether the business could be transferred or sold
