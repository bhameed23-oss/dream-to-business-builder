# Example Business Flow: Local Real Estate Business

This is a sample lifecycle flow for a local real estate or property-services business.

## Example scenario

A founder wants to run a real estate lead and listing business.

### Creation
- define the customer segment and trust strategy
- map how leads will enter the business
- identify compliance and privacy concerns
- build a minimal website and lead capture flow

### Operation
- track leads and follow-up efforts
- manage property intake and customer qualification
- manage appointments and showing coordination
- track follow-up patterns closely

### Auditing
- review lead quality and follow-up consistency
- review whether suspicious or unqualified leads are being handled properly
- review if customer information is stored and shared responsibly
- review how marketing and communication are being managed

### Scaling
- add standardized workflows for property intake
- improve lead scoring and CRM consistency
- review team documentation and handoff
- improve service reliability and follow-up quality

### Compliance Catch-Up
- review privacy, data retention, and communication permissions
- review record access and customer data handling
- review payment and contract communication rules if used

### Automation & Systems Upgrade
- automate lead capture and follow-up sequences
- standardize property notes and client communication
- connect booking or scheduling tools
- use AI to summarize lead notes and meeting outputs

### Exit Readiness
- document the sales or rental process
- identify how much value is tied to founder relationships
- review whether a buyer could run the business without the founder
- review customer and property records for clarity and transferability
