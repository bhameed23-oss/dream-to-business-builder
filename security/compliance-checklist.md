# Compliance Checklist

This checklist is meant to help users identify whether they need to take compliance or professional review seriously.

## Quick questions

- Are you collecting customer names, addresses, or contact details?
- Are you storing documents or files with personal information?
- Are you accepting payments or processing subscription billing?
- Are you storing sensitive business records or employee information?
- Are you operating in a regulated industry?
- Are you storing any healthcare or financial information?
- Are you dealing with customer records that may require consent or retention rules?
- Are you doing marketing or customer outreach by email or SMS?
- Are you selling regulated items or services?

## If you answered yes to any of the above

You should think seriously about:
- privacy policies
- secure storage and retention
- consent rules
- customer communication compliance
- legal or professional review

## Recommended review flow

1. Identify the information involved
2. Identify obligations by industry and geography
3. Review the system design and risk
4. Reduce collection of unnecessary data
5. Add safeguards
6. Keep documentation ready for review
7. Ask a lawyer or compliance professional for help if needed

## Strong warnings

Do not treat compliance as a small checklist. It can become serious fast if customer or employee data is mishandled.
