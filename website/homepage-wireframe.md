# Homepage Wireframe Structure

This file defines the initial homepage layout for the product.

## 1. Header
- logo / product name
- nav links: How it works, Lifecycle, Safety, Pricing or Community
- primary CTA: Start your review

## 2. Hero section
- headline
- subheadline
- primary CTA
- secondary CTA
- trust text or small supporting statement

## 3. Problem / value section
- businesses become chaotic without structure
- people need a clear view of the current phase
- growth without clarity creates risk
- risk without visibility creates avoidable problems

## 4. How it works
- choose your situation
- answer guided questions
- get your business profile
- see your lifecycle dashboard
- follow the next best actions

## 5. Lifecycle phases overview
- creation
- operation
- auditing
- scaling
- compliance catch-up
- automation & systems upgrade
- exit readiness

## 6. Safety and review section
- risk-aware guidance
- recommendations for professional review
- clarity around what should wait
- practical and not hype-driven

## 7. Benefits section
- clarity
- safer growth
- more structure
- better decision-making
- better workflow planning
- stronger business continuity

## 8. Final CTA section
- Start your business lifecycle review
- Get a clearer view of where your business is and what matters next

## 9. Footer
- links to docs, community, or project overview
- contact or contribution link
