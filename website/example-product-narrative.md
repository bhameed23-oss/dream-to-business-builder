# Example Product Narrative

This file provides a simple product story that can be used in marketing or site copy.

## Narrative

Most people do not need more generic business advice. They need a system that helps them think clearly about their business as it actually exists.

This project helps founders and small business owners move through the real lifecycle of a business: starting, operating, reviewing, scaling, fixing gaps, automating, and preparing for the future.

It brings together business clarity, operations, risk awareness, customer journey thinking, tool integration, and AI-assisted guidance in a way that is supportive, practical, and safer than ad hoc planning.

It is designed for people who are overwhelmed, missing structure, or unsure where to begin.

## Message

This is not just a business plan tool. It is a business lifecycle operating system for real people building real businesses.
