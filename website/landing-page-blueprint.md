# Landing Page Blueprint

This file defines the initial public-facing landing page for the product.

## Hero section

Headline:
- Business clarity for every stage of growth

Subheadline:
- A lifecycle operating system for founders and small business owners who need a practical path through business creation, operations, growth, risk, automation, and exit planning.

Primary CTA:
- Start your business lifecycle review

Secondary CTA:
- Learn how it works

## Supporting value section

### The problem
- businesses become chaotic over time
- founders often lack structure and clarity
- growth creates hidden risk and operational gaps
- automation without a clear system creates confusion

### The solution
- identify the current lifecycle phase
- review the health of the business
- get the next best actions
- keep the same business profile as the business evolves

## How it works

1. Choose your situation
2. Answer a short intake
3. Get your business profile
4. See your current phase and dashboard
5. Get a clear plan for next steps

## Benefits grid

- clearer direction
- safer growth
- fewer bottlenecks
- risk awareness
- smarter automation
- better exit readiness

## Trust and safety section

- explains that the framework supports human judgement
- recommends professional review when needed
- avoids hype and overpromises
- emphasizes practical structure over technical complexity

## Final CTA

- Start your business lifecycle review
- Build a clearer path for your business
