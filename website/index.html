Safety and compliance

Important: this project helps you build more safely, not recklessly.

It is designed to educate, guide, and organize the business-building process. It does not replace legal counsel, compliance review, insurance guidance, or specialized professional advice. If you are handling sensitive customer data, payments, regulated information, or risky workflows, you should review with a professional before launch.
