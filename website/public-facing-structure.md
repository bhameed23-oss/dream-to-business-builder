# Public-Facing Website Example Structure

This file outlines a practical website structure for the public-facing product experience.

## Suggested sections

### Hero section
- headline
- subheading
- CTA buttons
- optional trust message

### Benefits section
- business creation support
- operations and workflow clarity
- safety and compliance review
- scaling and automation planning
- exit readiness and transfer planning

### How it works
- choose your business phase
- answer a few questions
- review your business profile and dashboard
- get the right next actions

### Paths section
- beginner path
- operator path
- growth and scaling path
- compliance and risk path
- exit readiness path

### Safety section
- important review warnings
- human review recommendations
- privacy and compliance emphasis

### Contribution section
- open-source and community model
- people who can contribute
- why the project exists

## Why it matters

The public-facing structure should make the product feel useful and trustworthy, not complicated or vague.
