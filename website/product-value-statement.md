# Product Value Statement

This project helps people move from business chaos to business clarity.

It is designed for founders, operators, and small business owners who want a practical path through the real lifecycle of a business without needing to know all the technical, legal, or operational language in advance.

The value is simple:
- clearer direction
- better decision-making
- safer growth
- better workflows
- more realistic multi-stage business planning
- more confidence in what to do next

## Core message

The product helps users understand their business as a system and respond to it intelligently over time.
