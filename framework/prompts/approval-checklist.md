# Approval Checklist

This file can be used as a human approval checkpoint before major steps.

## Approval should happen before:

- implementing workflows
- collecting customer data at scale
- processing payments
- sending broad communications
- launching publicly
- using AI to take action without human oversight

## User review checklist

- Do I understand the business problems this is solving?
- Do I understand the risks and tradeoffs?
- Do I know what data is being collected or used?
- Do I understand the cost and complexity?
- Do I know whether I need a professional review?
- Do I agree with the recommended plan?
- Am I comfortable moving forward?

## Required step

If the answer is no to any major item, the project should pause and explain what is missing.
