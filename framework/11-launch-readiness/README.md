# Launch Readiness

This module helps the user determine whether the business is ready to go live.

## Purpose

Before launch, the user should know whether they are ready to do the following:

- launch a new website
- start booking customers
- send outreach
- collect customer information
- accept payments
- process documents or forms
- automate communication flows
- expand operations

## Launch readiness categories

### 1. Business clarity
- Does the business have a clear offer?
- Does the customer know what to do next?
- Is the value clear and easy to understand?

### 2. System readiness
- Are forms and workflows working?
- Are contact and scheduling processes clear?
- Are emails, messages, and follow-up ready?

### 3. Design readiness
- Does the business look trustworthy?
- Is the experience easy to use?
- Is the site accessible and mobile-friendly?

### 4. Security and privacy readiness
- Are security basics in place?
- Is HTTPS enabled?
- Are sensitive records protected?
- Are tools vetted?

### 5. Compliance readiness
- Is there a legal or risk review pending?
- Are there document policies or consent needs?
- Is there a professional review process in place if required?

## Launch gate

Every launch should include a personal review and a technical review.

### Launch gate questions

- Are there any data or customer privacy problems?
- Have we reviewed the customer journey and message flow?
- Are we sending messages that need consent or disclosure?
- Are we collecting too much information too early?
- Is the system secure enough for launch?
- Do we need a lawyer or compliance review before launch?

## Strong warning

The framework should not encourage rushing to launch. It should encourage thoughtful launch with review and monitoring.

## Launch summary output

The launch readiness phase should produce:

- ready to launch
- ready with caution
- not ready yet
- professional review required before launch
- next steps and timeline
