# Starter Product Flow

This file outlines the first product-like sequence a user will experience.

## Sequence

1. Choose your starting situation
2. Answer a few guided questions
3. Review your business profile summary
4. See your current phase and dashboard
5. Review the phase-specific output
6. Identify what to do next
7. Review risk or compliance if triggered
8. Save progress and continue later

## Why this is the right first flow

It is simple, useful, and grounded in the real lifecycle instead of abstract advice.

## Core principle

The product should always answer three questions clearly:
- where are we?
- what matters most right now?
- what should we do next?
