# Beginner Path

This path is for people who are just starting and may be overwhelmed.

## Beginner path goals

- reduce overwhelm
- explain things plainly
- keep tasks small
- focus on essentials first
- help people feel capable

## Beginner path structure

### Step 1: Understand the idea
- What is the business idea?
- What problem does it solve?
- Who is it for?

### Step 2: Review the basics
- customer type
- offer
- business structure
- risk and data handling

### Step 3: Create a simple plan
- what do you need first?
- what matters most right now?
- what can wait?

### Step 4: Build safe and simple
- choose the simplest version of the system
- avoid overbuilding
- add complexity later after learning

### Step 5: Review and improve
- track what works
- adjust and improve
- keep systems manageable

## Tone

The beginner path should feel supportive and calm. It should never shame or confuse users.
