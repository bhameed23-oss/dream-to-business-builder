# Lifecycle Business Health Scorecard

This file defines a simple health scorecard that can be used across the product.

## Scorecard categories
- business clarity
- customer experience
- operational health
- risk and compliance posture
- growth readiness
- automation maturity
- exit readiness

## Scoring scale
1 = weak or missing
2 = fragile
3 = functional but inconsistent
4 = strong and repeatable
5 = highly mature and resilient

## Example scorecard
```text
Business clarity: 4
Customer experience: 3
Operational health: 2
Risk and compliance posture: 2
Growth readiness: 2
Automation maturity: 3
Exit readiness: 1
```

## Why it matters

A scorecard helps the user understand what is actually working and what needs attention before moving forward.
