# Lifecycle Flow Overview

This file gives a practical view of how a business moves through the lifecycle.

## Typical journey

### New business
- Creation
- Operation
- Auditing
- Scaling
- Compliance Catch-Up when needed
- Automation & Systems Upgrade
- Exit Readiness only when relevant

### Existing business
- Auditing
- Operation review
- Compliance Catch-Up if needed
- Automation & Systems Upgrade
- Scaling
- Exit Readiness if the owner wants to sell, transfer, or prepare for investment

### Growing business
- Operation review
- Scaling
- Systems upgrade
- Compliance review
- Exit preparation if strategic change is planned

## Core idea

Not every business passes through every phase in the same order. The framework should help identify the current phase and the next most important actions.

## Recommended output

The system should always help the user answer:

- What phase are we in?
- What matters most right now?
- What are the main risks or bottlenecks?
- What should happen next?
- What needs professional review before we proceed?
