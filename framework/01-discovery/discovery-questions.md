# Framework Discovery Questions

This file contains example discovery questions to help users participate.

## Basic business questions

- What business are you building or improving?
- Who are your customers?
- What problem do you solve?
- What do you sell or provide?
- Where do you operate?
- What makes your business different?
- What is your current process today?

## Operational questions

- What tasks take too much time?
- Where do things get lost?
- What do customers ask repeatedly?
- What information do you collect manually?
- What tools do you use already?

## Risk & compliance questions

- Do you collect customer contact details?
- Do you accept payments?
- Do you store documents or identification information?
- Do you have any sensitive or regulated information?
- Do you plan to market by email or SMS?
- Do you need help from a professional for compliance review?

## Design questions

- What kind of impression do you want to create?
- What values or style do you want your business to communicate?
- Who are you trying to impress with the customer experience?
