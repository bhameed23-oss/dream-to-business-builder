# Business Intake Questionnaire

This is a structured questionnaire that can be used at the beginning of the process.

## Business Intake Questions

### Core business profile
- What type of business are you building or improving?
- Is this a new business or an existing business?
- What problem do you solve for your customers?
- Who are your ideal customers?
- What makes your business different?

### Offer and operations
- What do you sell or provide?
- What is your current process or workflow?
- Where does the client or customer experience break down?
- What has been done manually that could be standardized?

### Customer and marketing
- How do customers find you today?
- How do customers contact you or book services?
- What is the typical customer journey?
- What do customers ask most often?

### Current systems and tools
- What tools do you currently use?
- What is working well?
- What feels chaotic or inconsistent?
- What is difficult to track right now?

### Data and risk
- What information do you collect from customers or clients?
- What personal or sensitive data might you store?
- Do you accept payments or collect financial information?
- Are you in an industry with special rules or compliance requirements?

### Goals
- What would success look like in 90 days?
- What would you like to automate first?
- What would you like to reduce or remove from your workload?
- What would you most like help with right now?

## Output of this questionnaire

The system should produce:

- a business summary
- a risk flag summary
- a likely next steps path
- a priority list
- an explanation of what is missing
