# Product and Release Strategy

This file outlines the strategic direction for turning the lifecycle framework into a real product and launch path.

## Product thesis

People do not need another generic business template. They need a business operating system that helps them move through the real lifecycle of a business with clarity, control, and safety.

## Product categories

### 1. Core platform
A guided system that helps users:
- identify their business phase
- assess business health
- review risks and opportunities
- follow the next best steps
- preserve a shared business profile over time

### 2. Business lifecycle modules
The platform includes hands-on modules for:
- creation
- operation
- auditing
- scaling
- compliance catch-up
- automation & systems upgrade
- exit readiness

### 3. Tool connectivity layer
This layer helps users connect:
- APIs
- CLIs
- automation tools
- local AI and workflow tools
- existing business software

### 4. Human review and expert handoff
The system should recommend professional review when a business reaches a high-risk or high-complexity point.

## Why this matters

It gives the product a real market angle. It is not just a business toolkit; it is a structured support system for people who feel lost, overloaded, or uncertain about their next step.

## Suggested release stages

### Stage 1: MVP
- lifecycle intake
- business profile creation
- dashboard
- creation phase
- operation phase
- auditing phase

### Stage 2: Growth features
- scaling phase
- compliance catch-up phase
- automation and systems upgrade phase
- integration layer

### Stage 3: Advanced support
- exit readiness
- local AI and workflow connectors
- more detailed health scoring
- business path summaries by industry

### Stage 4: Community and open source expansion
- contributor guides
- example business flows
- public templates and walkthroughs
- more specialized modules

## Launch positioning

The product should be positioned as:
- a business lifecycle operating system
- a practical guide for founders and business owners
- a safety-aware business health and growth platform
- a system that helps people see what matters next

## Key principle

The product should feel like guidance and support, not pressure or buzzword-heavy fluff.
