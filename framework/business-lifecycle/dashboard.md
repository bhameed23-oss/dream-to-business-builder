# Business Lifecycle Dashboard

This dashboard is the central summary view for the business lifecycle framework.

## Purpose

The dashboard helps a user see their business as a system in motion rather than as disconnected tasks or isolated issues.

## Dashboard sections

### 1. Business snapshot
- business name
- business type
- stage
- owner or founder
- location or service area
- current business health summary

### 2. Current lifecycle phase
- creation
- operation
- auditing
- scaling
- compliance catch-up
- automation & systems upgrade
- exit readiness

### 3. Current priorities
- biggest operational bottleneck
- biggest safety or compliance concern
- biggest growth blocker
- biggest automation opportunity
- biggest risk to launch or scale

### 4. Risk summary
- customer data risk
- payment risk
- privacy risk
- legal or compliance review trigger
- founder dependency risk
- business continuity risk

### 5. Recommended next actions
- immediate actions
- 30-day actions
- 90-day actions
- possible professional review needs

### 6. Tool and integration summary
- tools in use
- connected systems
- missing integrations
- automation readiness
- AI helper or local model readiness

### 7. Business health score
A simple ongoing score can help track improvement over time.

Suggested categories:
- clarity
- operations
- customer experience
- risk control
- automation maturity
- scale readiness
- exit readiness

Each category can be scored from 1 to 5.

## Dashboard output

This should produce a simple summary that helps the user answer:

- Where are we right now?
- What should we fix next?
- What is safe to do now?
- What needs professional review?
- What should wait until later?

## Why it matters

A dashboard creates continuity and makes the business feel understandable. It also helps users see that actions in one phase affect other phases.
