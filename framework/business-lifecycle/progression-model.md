# Business Lifecycle Progression Model

This model explains the relationship between the phases and how they feed into each other.

## Core progression logic

- Creation sets the foundation and the initial risk boundaries
- Operation reveals fragility, bottlenecks, and process gaps
- Auditing identifies the real business health state
- Scaling depends on system maturity and consistent quality
- Compliance Catch-Up is often necessary once a business has real customer or financial activity
- Automation & Systems Upgrade comes after process clarity and risk review
- Exit Readiness depends on consistency, documentation, and reduced founder dependency

## System principle

Each lifecycle phase should improve clarity and reduce operational risk before the next phase begins.

## Business effect

If a business skips a phase or tries to grow before fixing the earlier structural issues, the quality, trust, and risk profile often worsen.

## Use in the framework

The progression model helps the system guide a user away from trying to scale, automate, or exit too early.
