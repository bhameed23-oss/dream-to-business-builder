# Creation Phase Checklist

Use this after reading README.md in this folder. Work through the items in order — each one builds on the last.

## 1. Clarify the idea

- [ ] Write one sentence that says what your business does and who it's for. (Example: "We repair collision damage for local car owners who need fast, honest estimates.")
- [ ] Write down the one problem your business solves. If you can't say it in one sentence, the idea is still too fuzzy to build on yet.
- [ ] List 3 people (real or realistic) who would pay for this. What do they have in common?

## 2. Define the simplest version

- [ ] Describe the smallest version of your business that could still deliver value to a customer today. Cut out anything that isn't essential.
- [ ] List what you will NOT do yet (extra services, extra locations, extra product lines). Saying no early prevents chaos later.

## 3. Map the first customer interaction

- [ ] Write down every step a customer goes through, from finding you to paying you.
- [ ] Identify which of those steps you'll do manually at first (this is normal — don't automate before you understand the process).

## 4. Flag early risk

- [ ] Will you collect any customer information (name, email, phone, payment)? If yes, note it — this becomes important in the Compliance phase later.
- [ ] Are there any licenses, insurance, or permits required for this type of business in your state or city?
- [ ] Is there anything about this business you're unsure is legal or allowed? Write it down so you remember to check.

## 5. Set your first-30-days plan

- [ ] Pick 1-3 concrete actions you'll take in the next 30 days (not 20 — pick a few and actually do them).
- [ ] Decide how you'll know if the first version is working (a number, a signal, a feeling from customers).

## Done when

You can describe your business in one sentence, you know your first customer step-by-step, and you've flagged anything that needs a professional's eyes before you go further.
