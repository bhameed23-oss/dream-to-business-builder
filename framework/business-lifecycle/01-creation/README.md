# Creation Phase

This phase is about helping a person turn an idea into a clear, structured, and safe starting point.

## Purpose

The creation phase answers:

- What business are we actually building?
- Who is it for?
- What problem does it solve?
- What needs to happen first?
- What is the simplest useful version of the business?
- What risk is present before launch?

## Core outputs

The creation phase should produce:

- business summary
- customer summary
- offer summary
- initial risk review
- launch boundaries
- first phase plan
- approval-ready outline

## Questions to guide the user

- What kind of business are you building or improving?
- What problem do you solve?
- Who benefits most?
- What do you currently do manually?
- What is the simplest minimal version of this business?
- What information will you collect from customers or clients?
- What would be risky or inappropriate to do before reviewing professional guidance?

## Design principle

Start with clarity, not complexity.

The goal is not to build everything immediately. The goal is to build the right first layer.

## Review gate

Before the user moves beyond the creation phase, they should review:

- the customer summary
- the risk summary
- the simplest first version of the business
- the necessary launch boundaries
- whether a professional review is needed
