# Creation Phase Resources

Real, free places to go for help in this phase. This tool gives you structure — these give you expert backup.

## Business basics

- **[SBA.gov - Plan Your Business](https://www.sba.gov/business-guide/plan-your-business)** — U.S. Small Business Administration's free guide to writing a business plan, choosing a structure, and calculating startup costs.
- **[SCORE](https://www.score.org)** — Free one-on-one mentoring from experienced business owners and executives. Available in most U.S. cities.
- **[SBA Local Assistance Finder](https://www.sba.gov/local-assistance)** — Find your nearest Small Business Development Center for free, in-person help.

## Legal structure & licenses

- **[SBA - Choose a Business Structure](https://www.sba.gov/business-guide/launch-your-business/choose-business-structure)** — Compares LLC, sole proprietorship, corporation, and partnership in plain language.
- **Your state's Secretary of State website** — Search "[your state] secretary of state business registration" to find your state's exact filing requirements.
- **Your city or county clerk's office** — For local business licenses and permits, which the SBA site won't cover.

## Customer discovery

- **[SBA - Market Research](https://www.sba.gov/business-guide/plan-your-business/market-research-competitive-analysis)** — How to research whether people will actually pay for what you're building.

## When to bring in a professional

Talk to a lawyer or accountant before you launch if:
- You'll be collecting sensitive customer data (health, financial, or government ID information)
- You're forming a partnership and splitting ownership or profit
- Your industry requires a specific license (contracting, food service, healthcare, financial services, etc.)
