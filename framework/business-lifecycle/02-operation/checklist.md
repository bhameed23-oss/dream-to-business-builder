# Operation Phase Checklist

Use this after reading README.md in this folder. This phase is about making a running business less chaotic and more repeatable.

## 1. Map what's actually happening today

- [ ] Write down your core process, step by step, exactly as it happens today (not how it's "supposed" to happen).
- [ ] Circle the steps that only you (the owner) can do. These are your bottlenecks.
- [ ] Circle the steps that get skipped or forgotten sometimes. These are your risk points.

## 2. Find the chaos

- [ ] List 3 things that go wrong or get missed most often (a follow-up that doesn't happen, a step that gets skipped when you're busy, etc.).
- [ ] For each one, write down why it happens — usually it's "I forget," "no one else knows how," or "there's no clear step for it."

## 3. Reduce manual work

- [ ] Pick ONE repetitive task you do by hand every week and write down exactly what it involves.
- [ ] Decide if it should be: documented (so someone else can do it), simplified (so it takes less time), or automated (so it happens without you).

## 4. Improve customer follow-up

- [ ] Write down what happens after a customer says yes. Is there a consistent next step, or does it depend on how busy you are?
- [ ] Pick one follow-up step to make consistent this month (a reminder, a check-in, a thank-you).

## 5. Set your operating rhythm

- [ ] Pick one recurring check-in (weekly or monthly) where you look at what's working and what's not.
- [ ] Write down the 3 numbers or signals you'll look at each time (e.g., number of jobs completed, number of follow-ups missed, customer complaints).

## Done when

You have a written (even if simple) version of your core process, you know your top 3 breakdown points, and you have one recurring habit to check how things are running.
