# Operation Phase Resources

## Process documentation

- **[SBA - Manage Your Business](https://www.sba.gov/business-guide/manage-your-business)** — Free guides on staffing, operations, and day-to-day management.
- **Google Docs / Google Sheets** — Free, simple way to start writing down your process. Don't overthink the tool — a shared doc your team can read beats no documentation at all.

## Customer relationship tools (CRM)

If you're managing customer follow-up manually and it's slipping, a free-tier CRM can help before you pay for anything complex:
- **HubSpot Free CRM** — Free tier covers basic contact tracking and follow-up reminders.
- **Google Calendar + reminders** — If you're not ready for a CRM, structured calendar reminders are a real, working substitute.

## Learning operational discipline

- **[SCORE - Operations Templates](https://www.score.org/resource/template)** — Free templates for SOPs (standard operating procedures), scheduling, and workflow tracking.

## When to bring in a professional

Talk to an operations consultant or experienced advisor if:
- The same mistake keeps happening even after you've tried to fix the process
- You're about to hire your first employee and need help building repeatable training
- You genuinely don't know why something breaks down, only that it does
