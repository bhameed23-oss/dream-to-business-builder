# Operation Phase

This phase is about supporting a business that is already running or about to run.

## Purpose

The operation phase answers:

- How is the business currently operating?
- What is working well?
- What is fragile or chaotic?
- What is being handled manually?
- What is the daily operational bottleneck?

## Key areas to review

- customer flow
- lead flow
- appointment or booking workflow
- follow-up processes
- internal tasks and responsibility structure
- current systems and tools
- communication and notification flows
- human review requirements

## Goal

The operation phase should help the business reduce chaos and make the work easier to repeat, track, and improve.

## Recommended output

- current process summary
- bottleneck list
- strengths and weaknesses
- operational risk summary
- recommended next-step improvements
