# Business Lifecycle Overview

This directory is the heart of the project. It contains the full business lifecycle and includes each stage the business may move through.

## Lifecycle phases

- Creation
- Operation
- Auditing
- Scaling
- Compliance Catch-Up
- Automation & Systems Upgrade
- Exit Readiness

## Phase purpose

Each phase answers a different question:

- Creation: What is the business and what should be built first?
- Operation: How is the business being run today?
- Auditing: What is working, what is broken, and what is risky?
- Scaling: How does the business grow without falling apart?
- Compliance Catch-Up: What security, privacy, or legal gaps need fixing?
- Automation & Systems Upgrade: What can be automated or improved safely?
- Exit Readiness: Is the business ready for sale, investment, or transfer?

## Shared components

Each phase should include:

- overview
- key questions
- review framework
- risk and compliance considerations
- actions and recommendations
- approval checkpoints
- output summary

## Intention

The framework should act like a guided operating system for the business lifecycle rather than a static checklist.
