# Auditing Phase

This phase is about reviewing the business as a complete system.

## Purpose

The auditing phase answers:

- What is working?
- What is broken or inconsistent?
- What is missing?
- What risks and gaps exist across the business?
- Where do operations, design, security, and marketing fail to align?

## Audit categories

- customer experience
- operational process
- security and privacy
- legal and risk review needs
- marketing and messaging clarity
- brand and trust review
- development and tooling review
- automation opportunity review
- quality and reliability review

## Why it matters

A business can appear active while still being unstructured, unsafe, or hard to scale. This phase helps reveal the real state of the system.

## Output

- audit summary
- gap list
- process issues
- operational risks
- technical and design review notes
- recommended improvement priorities
