# Auditing Phase Checklist

Use this after reading README.md in this folder. This phase is a structured look under the hood before you grow, change, or fix anything.

## 1. Review each area honestly

Go category by category. For each one, write one line: "Working," "Shaky," or "Broken" — and one sentence why.

- [ ] Customer experience (Is it consistent? Would a customer notice if something went wrong?)
- [ ] Operational process (Does work flow smoothly, or does it depend on you remembering things?)
- [ ] Security and privacy (Do you know where customer data lives and who can see it?)
- [ ] Legal and risk (Are you covered — contracts, licenses, insurance — for what you're actually doing?)
- [ ] Marketing and messaging (Is it clear what you offer and why someone should choose you?)
- [ ] Brand and trust (Does your business look and feel as trustworthy as it actually is?)
- [ ] Tools and tech (Are your tools helping, or are they scattered and half-used?)
- [ ] Automation opportunity (What's still fully manual that doesn't need to be?)
- [ ] Quality and reliability (Do you deliver the same quality every time, or does it vary?)

## 2. Build your gap list

- [ ] From the categories marked "Shaky" or "Broken," list the top 3 gaps that would hurt you most if left alone for another year.
- [ ] For each gap, write down: what's the actual risk if this doesn't get fixed?

## 3. Prioritize

- [ ] Rank your gap list: which one is cheapest/fastest to fix, and which one is most urgent (not always the same thing)?
- [ ] Pick ONE to start on this month.

## Done when

You've honestly rated every category, you have a short list of real gaps (not everything — just the ones that matter), and you know which one you're tackling first.
