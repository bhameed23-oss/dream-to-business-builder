# Exit Readiness Checklist

Use this after reading README.md in this folder. This phase is about making the business easy to hand off — whether that's a sale, a succession, or bringing in a partner.

## 1. Check founder dependency

- [ ] List everything that only happens because you personally do it or know it.
- [ ] For each one, ask: "If I disappeared for a month, would this still work?" If not, that's a value blocker.

## 2. Review documentation

- [ ] Do you have written records of your core processes, or is it all in your head?
- [ ] Are your financial records organized and up to date (income, expenses, contracts)?

## 3. Review customer relationships

- [ ] What percentage of your revenue comes from your single biggest customer? (High concentration is a red flag to buyers.)
- [ ] Are customer relationships tied to you personally, or to the business as a whole?

## 4. Review legal and financial cleanliness

- [ ] Are your business finances separate from your personal finances?
- [ ] Are there any contracts, agreements, or legal loose ends that haven't been resolved?

## 5. Review systems and data

- [ ] Could someone else step in and understand your systems and records without you explaining everything verbally?
- [ ] Is customer and business data organized in a way a new owner could actually use?

## 6. Build your value-blocker list

- [ ] From everything above, list the top 3 things that would make a buyer, investor, or successor nervous.
- [ ] Pick the one that's most fixable and start there.

## Done when

You know exactly what depends on you personally, your records are organized enough for someone else to review, and you have a ranked list of what to fix before pursuing a sale, investment, or transition.
