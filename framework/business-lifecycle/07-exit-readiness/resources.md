# Exit Readiness Resources

## Selling or transferring a business

- **[SBA - Exit Your Business](https://www.sba.gov/business-guide/exit-or-close-your-business)** — Federal overview of selling, transferring, or closing a business.
- **SCORE mentors** — Many have personally sold businesses and can give you a realistic read on your readiness before you talk to a broker.

## Valuation

- **A business broker or M&A advisor** — For an actual valuation, this is worth paying for rather than guessing. Search "business broker [your industry] [your state]."
- **[IBBA (International Business Brokers Association)](https://www.ibba.org/)** — Directory to find a credentialed business broker.

## Legal and financial preparation

- **A business attorney** — To review contracts, ownership structure, and any legal loose ends before a sale or transfer conversation starts.
- **A CPA or accountant** — Buyers will want clean, organized financials. If yours aren't there yet, this is the person to fix that with.

## When to bring in a professional

This entire phase benefits from professional involvement, but especially:
- Before you have any serious conversation with a buyer or investor
- Before you sign anything (letters of intent, NDAs, purchase agreements)
- If you're unsure what your business is actually worth
