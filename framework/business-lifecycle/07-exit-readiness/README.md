# Exit Readiness Phase

This phase helps the business owner assess whether the business is ready to be sold, transferred, or prepared for investment or strategic change.

## Purpose

The exit phase answers:

- Is the business actually saleable?
- Is it investor-ready?
- What would a buyer need to see?
- What weaknesses would reduce value?
- Are the systems, records, and operations mature enough?

## Exit readiness categories

- business health and operating stability
- customer concentration and retention
- process documentation
- team dependency on the founder
- financial clarity
- legal and compliance posture
- data and system cleanliness
- marketability and positioning

## Important note

A business may be profitable but still not be exit-ready. Some businesses are not easy to transfer or sell because they depend too much on the founder, are poorly documented, or lack reliable systems.

## Output

- exit readiness summary
- value blockers
- required internal improvements
- sale or investor-readiness assessment
- recommended next steps
