# Compliance Catch-Up Phase

This phase exists for businesses that have grown without properly reviewing the legal, privacy, or security implications of what they are doing.

## Purpose

This phase answers:

- What compliance or risk issues do we need to catch up on?
- What customer data are we handling?
- What processes may be risky or non-compliant?
- Where do we owe a professional review?

## Typical issues to review

- customer contact information
- payment processing
- document handling
- communications and marketing permission
- privacy and storage practices
- access control and authentication
- third-party tools and connections
- policy gaps and documentation gaps

## Important reminder

This phase is not a replacement for legal or compliance counsel. It is a structured way to identify and understand the problem before professional review.

## Output

- compliance gap summary
- data handling review
- risk exposure review
- remediation priorities
- recommended professional review list
