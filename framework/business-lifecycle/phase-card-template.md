# Phase Card Template

This file is the reusable template for each lifecycle phase.

## Card structure

### Phase name
- creation
- operation
- auditing
- scaling
- compliance catch-up
- automation & systems upgrade
- exit readiness

### Purpose
A one-paragraph explanation of the phase.

### Questions to answer
- What is the central business question for this phase?
- What is the user trying to determine right now?
- What is missing or risky?

### Review areas
- operations
- customer experience
- design and trust
- risk and compliance
- tools and integrations
- growth or transition impact

### Action list
- immediate actions
- recommended next steps
- what should be delayed
- what needs professional review

### Output summary
- summary statement
- key risks
- next best move
- owner approval status

## Why this template matters

A consistent phase card format keeps the framework structured, repeatable, and easier to expand over time.
