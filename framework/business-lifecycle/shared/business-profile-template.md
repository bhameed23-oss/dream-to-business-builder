# Business Profile Template

This template helps store and carry the shared business context across phases.

## Template sections

- business name
- business type
- stage
- industry
- customers
- offer
- pricing or revenue model
- customer journey
- operational model
- current tools
- security and privacy summary
- compliance review status
- growth stage
- automation opportunities
- exit goals

## Goal

To create one source of truth for the business across the lifecycle.
