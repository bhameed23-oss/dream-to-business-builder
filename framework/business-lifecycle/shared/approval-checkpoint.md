# Approval Checkpoint

This shared checkpoint ensures no critical business decision is made without human review.

## Checklist

- Have we understood the business and the current phase?
- Have we identified the main risk and the likely cost of failure?
- Have we reviewed the likely next steps carefully?
- Is the business ready for the proposed decision?
- Do we need professional review before moving forward?

## Rule

If the answer is unclear, the system should pause and explain what is missing.
