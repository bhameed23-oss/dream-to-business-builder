# Risk Review Checklist

This is a shared checklist for reviewing risk across the lifecycle.

## Checklist

- Are you collecting customer or business data?
- Are you storing personally identifiable or sensitive information?
- Are you sending messages or outreach to customers?
- Are you accepting payments?
- Are you storing files or documents?
- Are you using third-party tools?
- Are you handling regulated information?
- Do you need a professional review?
- Are there operational or business risks unique to this stage?

## Rule

If the answer is yes to a risky category, the framework should recommend review before moving forward.
