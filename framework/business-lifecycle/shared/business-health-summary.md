# Business Health Summary

This file is used to summarize the overall health of the business across the lifecycle.

## Summary categories

### 1. Business clarity
- clear offer
- clear target customer
- clear market position
- clear value proposition

### 2. Customer experience
- easy to understand process
- clear communications
- consistent follow-up
- trust-building customer experience

### 3. Internal operations
- defined roles
- repeatable process
- manageable workload
- fewer obvious bottlenecks

### 4. Risk and compliance
- known customer data handling
- known privacy and security gaps
- professional review triggers identified
- policies or controls lacking or in place

### 5. Growth readiness
- business can handle more volume
- systems support scale
- quality remains consistent
- roles are clear enough to delegate

### 6. Automation readiness
- repetitive tasks identified
- automation opportunities known
- key workflows documented
- safe automation path defined

### 7. Exit readiness
- business is transferable
- founder dependency is reduced
- records and processes are documented
- the business is explainable and sellable

## Suggested score model

Score each category from 1 to 5 and summarize the overall health.

## Example output

```text
Business Health Summary

Clarity: 4/5
Customer Experience: 3/5
Internal Operations: 2/5
Risk and Compliance: 2/5
Growth Readiness: 2/5
Automation Readiness: 3/5
Exit Readiness: 1/5
```

## Why it matters

It gives the user a clear picture of what is strong and what is weak before they try to scale or automate.
