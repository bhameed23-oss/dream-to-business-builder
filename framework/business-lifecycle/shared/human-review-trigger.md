# Human Review Trigger

This shared trigger helps identify when the system should recommend professional or human review.

## Trigger examples

- customer data stored without basic safeguards
- payment processing involved
- regulated information present
- significant legal or contractual obligations
- large-scale automation touching sensitive workflows
- business is being prepared for sale or investment
- the founder is highly dependent on personal memory or undocumented processes

## Rule

When these situations arise, the framework should recommend review by a qualified professional instead of suggesting the user proceed on their own.
