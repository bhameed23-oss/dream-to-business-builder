# Scaling Phase Resources

## Growth planning

- **[SBA - Grow Your Business](https://www.sba.gov/business-guide/grow-your-business)** — Free federal guide covering hiring, expanding, and funding growth.
- **[SCORE - Growth Mentoring](https://www.score.org)** — Free mentors who have personally scaled businesses can sanity-check your growth plan before you commit money to it.

## Hiring and delegation

- **[SBA - Hire and Manage Employees](https://www.sba.gov/business-guide/manage-your-business/hire-manage-employees)** — Covers the legal basics of bringing on your first employees or contractors.
- **[IRS - Independent Contractor vs Employee](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/independent-contractor-self-employed-or-employee)** — Critical to get right before you delegate work; misclassifying workers is a common and expensive mistake.

## Funding growth

- **[SBA - Loans](https://www.sba.gov/funding-programs/loans)** — Official overview of SBA-backed small business loan programs.

## When to bring in a professional

Talk to an accountant or business advisor before scaling if:
- Growth would require taking on debt or outside investment
- You're about to hire your first W-2 employee (payroll taxes and compliance get more complex fast)
- The growth would significantly change how much customer data you're handling
