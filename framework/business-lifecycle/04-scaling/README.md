# Scaling Phase

This phase is about helping a business grow without breaking its core systems.

## Purpose

The scaling phase answers:

- How can the business grow without becoming chaotic?
- What systems need to be stronger before scale?
- What processes are resilient enough for growth?
- What needs to be standardized or delegated?

## Key questions

- What is causing stress when the business gets busier?
- What is difficult to scale manually?
- What should be automated, standardized, or delegated?
- What would a bigger volume of work do to operations, quality, and customer experience?
- Do we need better team roles, policies, or operating systems?

## Outputs

- growth readiness review
- scale blockers
- staffing and process needs
- automation opportunities
- risk review tied to growth
- recommended scale plan
