# Full Business Journey Flow

This file describes the end-to-end journey a business can take through the lifecycle.

## Journey overview

1. Creation
   - define the idea
   - define the customer
   - define the offer
   - assess risk before launch
   - identify the minimum viable operating system

2. Operation
   - run the business day to day
   - track bottlenecks and service quality
   - review recurring operational problems
   - reduce chaos and improve repeatability

3. Auditing
   - review all areas of the business
   - identify gaps across operations, design, risk, and growth
   - compare what is working with what is fragile

4. Scaling
   - improve system stability before growth
   - add clearer roles and workflows
   - identify what should be automated or delegated
   - build better quality control and process maturity

5. Compliance Catch-Up
   - identify legal or privacy gaps
   - review customer data handling and contract obligations
   - decide what needs professional review

6. Automation & Systems Upgrade
   - improve workflows and reduce repeated manual work
   - add safer integrations and better connected tools
   - move from fragile systems to more repeatable systems

7. Exit Readiness
   - assess whether the business is transfer-ready or investable
   - document the operating system
   - reduce founder dependence
   - understand the sale or transfer story

## Why this matters

The system should help the user understand that businesses do not move in one straight line. They move in cycles, and each phase affects the next one.

## Core question for the user

What phase are we in right now, and what is the next best step for this phase?
