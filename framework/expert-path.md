# Expert Path

This path is for people with more experience, more operational complexity, or a larger business need.

## Expert path goals

- manage deeper complexity
- identify risk earlier
- create more detailed operational design
- decide what to automate and what to keep human-led
- support more advanced growth planning

## Expert path structure

### Step 1: Business model review
- market and positioning
- key offer
- segmentation
- channel strategy
- growth plan

### Step 2: Operations review
- internal processes
- bottlenecks
- automation opportunities
- process gaps
- service design

### Step 3: Risk and security review
- data handling
- customer flows
- communication rules
- payment and compliance risk
- professional review triggers

### Step 4: System architecture plan
- CRM, booking, forms, messaging, automation, and tooling
- review of tool stack and architecture
- plan for deployment and scaling

### Step 5: Launch and continuous improvement
- stage the launch
- measure performance
- evaluate reliability and trust
- improve over time

## Tone

The expert path should be clear, structured, and confident without ignoring safety or legal responsibility.
