# Review and Improvement

This module keeps the business system alive after launch.

## Purpose

A business system is not a one-time build. It should improve as the business grows and as the market shifts.

This phase helps the founder review:

- what is working
- what is too manual
- what is wasting time
- what is creating friction
- what needs to be improved next

## Questions to ask regularly

- What is causing confusion for customers?
- What is causing stress for staff?
- What is expensive or time-consuming to maintain?
- Are there missed opportunities for automation?
- Are security and compliance guidelines still being followed?
- Are there new risks that emerged as the business grew?

## Key review cycles

### Monthly review
- lead flow and responsiveness
- messaging quality
- customer experience issues
- workflow bottlenecks

### Quarterly review
- strategy and goals
- automation opportunities
- security review
- compliance status
- design trust and clarity

### Annual review
- business model fit
- technology stack review
- legal and policy review
- strategic pivot opportunities
- growth planning

## Improvement mindset

The goal is not to build everything immediately. The goal is to make it easier to run the business as it grows with clear priorities and steady improvement.

## Suggested output

The review phase should produce:

- what improved
- what still needs work
- what is not working
- what to fix next
- what to automate next
- what needs professional review

## Guardrail phrase

> Improvement should be deliberate, not chaotic. High-risk change should be reviewed before launch or implementation.
