# Business System Check

This module helps existing businesses review what they already have and what is missing.

## Why this matters

Many businesses are working, but they are operating in ways that are hard to scale, hard to track, and hard to improve. A business can appear busy while still being inefficient, fragile, or difficult to grow.

This module is designed to help a founder or operator answer:

- What is working already?
- What is still manual?
- What is slowing the business down?
- What is missing from the customer experience?
- What is missing from the internal operations?
- What is important to automate first?
- What needs professional review before change?

## The business system check has five parts

### 1. Customer experience audit

- How do customers contact the business?
- What happens after they contact you?
- Is the process clear, consistent, and professional?
- What happens after the sale or service is complete?
- Is there a clear follow-up process?

### 2. Internal operations audit

- What is tracked manually?
- What gets lost or delayed?
- What tasks repeat often?
- Who handles what work?
- What does the business rely on memory for?

### 3. Growth readiness audit

- Is the business easy to scale?
- Is the website or messaging consistent?
- Are reviews and referrals working?
- Are there queues or bottlenecks?
- Can new customers be handled without chaos?

### 4. Risk and compliance audit

- What information do you collect?
- Where is it stored?
- Who has access?
- What documents are required?
- What legal or privacy issues need review?

### 5. Automation opportunity audit

- What tasks can be automated without losing quality?
- Which tasks create the most wasted time?
- Which tasks are repetitive and predictable?
- Which tasks are valuable to keep human-led?

## Recommended output

This module should produce a review summary with:

- positives
- pain points
- missing systems
- needed automation
- risk flags
- professional review triggers
- prioritized next steps

## Suggested review questions

- What is the single biggest time drain in your business?
- What task is most likely to create customer frustration?
- What information is currently stored in emails, texts, or notes?
- What things take longer than they should?
- What parts of the process feel inconsistent?
- What would make your business easier to run next month?

## Guardrail language

> This review helps you identify operational gaps, but it does not replace professional legal, compliance, or technical review where risk is present.

## Recommended use

This is especially helpful for:

- existing businesses trying to improve
- owners who feel overwhelmed by daily operations
- founders who need a structured review before building tools or automation
- teams who want to reduce chaos without complex systems
