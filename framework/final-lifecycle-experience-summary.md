# Final Lifecycle Experience Summary

This file summarizes the end-to-end lifecycle experience in plain language.

## User experience

A user begins by choosing the situation they are in:
- new idea or startup phase
- current business needing structure
- growth and scaling challenge
- compliance or risk review
- automation and workflow improvement
- exit or transfer planning

The system then:
1. gathers a small amount of context
2. creates a shared business profile
3. identifies the current lifecycle phase
4. shows a business health score
5. highlights risk and priorities
6. recommends the next best step
7. keeps the business context for future reviews

## Product promise

The user gets clarity, continuity, safer decision-making, and a realistic next step instead of vague inspiration or generic advice.

## Why it matters

A business is not static. It changes, grows, breaks, improves, and matures. This system helps the user understand that reality and respond to it with structure.
