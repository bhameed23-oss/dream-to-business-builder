# MVP User Flow

This file defines the minimal viable product flow for the business lifecycle system.

## Goal

Help a user identify their current phase, understand their business health, and get the next best actions without overwhelming them.

## Step 1: Choose business situation
The user selects one of the following:
- starting a new business
- improving an existing business
- growing a business
- fixing risk or compliance gaps
- automating and improving systems
- preparing to exit or transfer

## Step 2: Answer guided intake questions
A short intake collects:
- business concept
- current stage
- target customer
- main pain point
- risk concern
- tools currently used
- goal for the next 30 to 90 days

## Step 3: Build the business profile
The system creates a business profile with:
- business overview
- customer summary
- process summary
- risk summary
- tool overview
- current business health status

## Step 4: Recommend the phase
The system identifies the most useful lifecycle phase and shows the relevant dashboard and actions.

## Step 5: Show the dashboard
The dashboard includes:
- current phase
- business health score
- high-priority risks
- next actions
- suggested phase outputs
- tool and automation overview

## Step 6: Deliver phase output
The user receives a clear summary and action list from the chosen phase.

## Step 7: Continue over time
The business profile persists and updates through future reviews, growth, or exit decisions.

## Why it matters

This flow is simple enough for a user to start immediately but powerful enough to reveal the real business state and the next best move.
