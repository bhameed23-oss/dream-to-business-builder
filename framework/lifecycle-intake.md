# Lifecycle Intake

This file contains the top-level intake questions that help determine which lifecycle phase to start in.

## Core intake questions

- What is your current business situation?
- Are you building a new business or improving an existing one?
- What phase are you in right now?
- Are you focused on starting, operating, auditing, scaling, fixing compliance, automating, or preparing to exit?
- What are your current goals and biggest pain points?
- What is the biggest source of risk or uncertainty right now?
- What systems or tools are currently being used?
- What do you want the system to help you do first?

## Output

The intake should determine:

- the current lifecycle phase
- the most relevant modules
- the likely risks to review first
- the possible next steps
- whether to start with a beginner or expert path
