# Business Idea to Business Plan Output

This document is the final user-facing output that the system should help generate after the business intake and review phases.

## Purpose

This template turns the interview and assessment into a usable business plan that a founder can review, edit, and approve.

## Final Output Structure

### 1. Business Summary
- Business name
- Business type
- Business stage
- Business concept
- What the business does
- Who it serves
- What problem it solves

### 2. Business Model
- offer or service
- pricing model
- customer acquisition path
- repeat purchase or referral potential
- value proposition

### 3. Customer Journey
- how customers learn about the business
- how they contact or book
- how they receive service or product
- what happens after the sale
- what follow-up or retention process exists

### 4. Business Structure
- owner or founder structure
- roles needed
- responsibilities by role
- managing operations and tasks
- what is currently manual

### 5. Business Risk Review
- data collected
- customer privacy risks
- payment risks
- legal requirements
- compliance concerns
- what needs professional review

### 6. Design and Brand Direction
- brand mood
- visual style
- tone of voice
- trust-building cues
- how the business should feel to customers

### 7. Website and Customer Experience Plan
- landing page structure
- booking or contact process
- lead capture flow
- mobile-friendliness
- accessibility requirements
- trust and professionalism needs

### 8. Operations Workflow Plan
- booking process
- scheduling process
- reminder process
- sales or inquiry process
- follow-up process
- customer service flow
- internal roles and steps

### 9. Automation Opportunities
- what to automate now
- what to automate later
- what should stay human-led
- what requires review before automation

### 10. Launch Readiness Checklist
- security basics
- website readiness
- payment and privacy review
- legal review triggers
- launch approved or paused
- next timeline

### 11. Improvement Plan
- what to monitor over 30 days
- what to improve next
- what to test
- what to review monthly

## Example output

```text
Business Name: Bright Path Auto Care
Business Type: Auto body and cosmetic repair shop
Business Stage: New business
Value Proposition: Professional, trustworthy vehicle repair and cosmetic care for busy drivers.
Customer: Local drivers focused on convenience and communication.
Main Pain Points: Manual scheduling, inconsistent communication, missed updates, poor follow-up.
Primary Risk: Customer data, vehicle information, and insurance-related communication.
Recommended First Build: Booking flow + status update workflow + secure customer intake form + review request automation.
Professional Review Needed: Yes, for privacy, document handling, and communications.
```

## Final approval step

Before the user moves from planning to building, they should review the generated output and approve or revise it.

This keeps the framework human-centered and reduces risk.
