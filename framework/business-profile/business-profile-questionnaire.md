# Business Profile Questionnaire

This file captures the business context that flows across the entire lifecycle.

## Core business profile

### Identity
- Business name
- Business type
- Industry or niche
- Stage: new, early, steady, growing, mature, exit-ready
- Founder or owner names
- Location or service area

### Business concept
- What the business does
- What problem it solves
- Who it serves
- Why this business exists
- Current offer and value proposition

### Customer profile
- Ideal customer
- Typical customer pain points
- Customer journey
- Customer acquisition channels
- Referral or repeat-business patterns

### Operations
- Current workflow summary
- Key operational bottlenecks
- Manual tasks
- Tools currently in use
- Current business process map

### Risk and compliance
- What data is collected
- Customer information stored
- Payment or financial handling
- Sensitive documents or records
- Regulated information or industry-specific rules
- Current compliance state

### Growth and scale readiness
- Current business size
- Current revenue or lead patterns
- Growth goals
- Bottlenecks to scale
- Resource constraints

### Automation and tool stack
- Current software stack
- APIs and integrations in use
- Automation opportunities
- What should remain human-led
- Tool sprawl or inefficiency

### Exit readiness
- Is the business saleable?
- What would a buyer need to see?
- Are systems documented?
- Does the founder own the customer relationships or key process knowledge?
- What would block a transfer or sale?

## Output

The questionnaire should produce a shared business profile usable across all lifecycle phases.
