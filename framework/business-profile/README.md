# Business Profile

The business profile is a shared record that follows the user through every phase of the lifecycle.

## Purpose

A business profile helps the system carry context forward instead of starting from zero each time. The business profile is the central source of truth for the business across creation, operation, scaling, compliance, and exit.

## Example profile sections

- business name
- business type
- business stage
- founder or owner details
- target customers
- offer and pricing
- current process summary
- systems and tools used
- brand and messaging summary
- customer journey summary
- operational pain points
- risk and compliance summary
- growth goals
- automation opportunities
- exit goals

## Why it matters

This allows the system to:

- keep continuity across phases
- avoid repeating questions unnecessarily
- show the business as a connected system
- help a founder see how operations, risk, design, and growth all connect

## Potential use cases

- new business planning
- business auditing
- scaling analysis
- compliance reviews
- automation and systems upgrade planning
- investor or exit readiness review
