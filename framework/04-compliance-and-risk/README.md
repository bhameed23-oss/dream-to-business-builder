# Compliance and Risk Review

This phase is intentionally serious. It gives the user a practical, organized review of risk before they launch or automate a business system.

## Purpose

This phase protects the user from building systems that expose them to:

- personal data loss
- customer trust issues
- legal problems
- compliance violations
- payment risk
- expensive remediation work
- reputational damage

## Required review categories

- data collection
- customer contact and communication
- payment processing
- document and file storage
- employee or vendor data
- regulated information
- legal requirements by industry
- privacy and retention
- website and hosting security
- third-party tool risk

## Review flow

1. Identify the nature of the data involved
2. Check whether the business is in a regulated industry
3. Evaluate payment risk and customer information risk
4. Review communication risks like email or SMS outreach
5. Check whether legal or professional review is needed
6. Provide a structured checklist
7. Present a risk summary
8. Trigger recommendation to ask a lawyer or specialist when appropriate

## Openable gate model

This framework should only allow progression after the user has:

- read the risk summary
- acknowledged the risk
- reviewed the relevant checklist
- agreed to continue or pause

### Example gate language

> This phase identifies important business, privacy, and legal risks. You may need professional review before proceeding. Please review the guidance and acknowledge the risk before going forward.

This should be a two-step acknowledgment process.

## Important rule

The framework should never imply that it is legal advice or a replacement for professional legal review.

It should help people make better decisions and make professional review easier and more structured.

## Required output

The compliance phase should produce:

- risk summary
- compliance checklist
- data handling review
- missing documents or policies
- required professional review list
- final launch recommendation
- clear next steps
