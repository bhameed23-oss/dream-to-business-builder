# What Do You Need Right Now?

This is a quick decision tool for the first impression of the project.

## Goal

The user should be able to tell the system what they are trying to achieve and get a relevant starting point.

## Primary categories

### I want to start a business
- help me define the business
- identify the offer and customer
- create the first structure
- guide me through business setup

### I have an existing business and need help
- assess current operations
- find bottlenecks and gaps
- identify automation opportunities
- review risk and compliance issues

### I want a website and customer experience
- design the brand direction
- create the customer journey
- define the lead flow
- build trust and usability

### I want to automate work
- identify repetitive tasks
- design a workflow plan
- review tools and integrations
- identify what should remain human-led

### I need help with security and compliance
- understand what data I handle
- identify legal or regulatory risk
- find a structured review process
- know when to bring in a professional

## Why this matters

This helps the system avoid one-size-fits-all answers and gives the user a path quickly.

## Suggested output

Based on their answer, the system should provide:

- recommended path
- likely first steps
- relevant framework modules
- suggested business examples
- clear warnings if risk is high
