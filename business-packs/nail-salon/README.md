# Example Business Pack: Nail Salon

This is a simple example business pack to help demonstrate how the framework can work in practice.

## Business summary

A nail salon provides beauty and self-care services to local customers.

## Typical goals

- manage appointments
- send reminders
- track customer preferences
- automate follow-up messages
- collect consent for promotions
- maintain a clean customer record system
- manage staff schedules
- reduce missed appointments

## Example questions

- How do customers book appointments today?
- Do you have a website or social media presence?
- Do you use a scheduling tool or calendar?
- Do you send reminders?
- Do you collect customer contact information?
- Do you need pre-appointment forms or waivers?
- Do you want a customer journey that feels premium?

## Risk review

This business does not usually require advanced legal review unless it handles sensitive customer data, payment processing, or regulated services. It still should protect customer contact information and use reasonable security.

## Example plan

1. choose a booking tool or simple website
2. gather customer contact information safely
3. create reminders and follow-up messages
4. build a simple review or loyalty path
5. track staff schedule and capacity
6. review security and privacy basics
7. review customer experience and design
8. improve over time

## Example automation opportunities

- appointment reminders
- confirmation messages
- missed appointment follow-up
- repeat customer reminders
- social media content planning
- marketing templates
- review request automation
- staff scheduling support

## Example AI use cases

- answer common customer questions
- schedule appointments based on available slots
- generate marketing copy
- summarize customer preferences
- help build an onboarding flow for new clients
