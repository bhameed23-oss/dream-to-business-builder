# Nail Salon — Tools and Integrations

## Booking and scheduling

- **Square Appointments** — Free for a single location/limited use, paid tiers for more. Handles booking, reminders, and payment together — a strong first stop for a salon of any size.
- **Booksy** — Paid, popular specifically in the beauty/nail industry, includes a client-facing app many customers already have installed.
- **Calendly** — Free/low-cost, simpler option if you just need a booking link without full payment integration.

## Payments

- **Square** or **Clover** — Standard, secure card processing that also often bundles with the booking tools above.

## Marketing and loyalty

- **Instagram / TikTok Business accounts** (free) — For a visual business like this, your portfolio of work is your best marketing, and it costs nothing to post.
- **Square Loyalty** or similar built into your booking tool — simpler than running a separate loyalty program.

## Reviews

- **Google Business Profile** (free) — Set this up if you haven't. It's often the first thing a potential customer checks.

## A note on customer data

Even though this business usually doesn't handle highly sensitive data, customer contact info and payment details still deserve basic care: use a real payment processor rather than storing card numbers yourself, and only use customer contact info for marketing if they've actually agreed to it.
