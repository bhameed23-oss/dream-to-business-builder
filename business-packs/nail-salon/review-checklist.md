# Nail Salon — Review Checklist

## Booking and scheduling
- [ ] Customers can book without needing to call during business hours
- [ ] Reminders go out before appointments (text or email)
- [ ] There's a clear no-show policy, even if it's simple

## Customer data
- [ ] Customer contact info is stored in one place, not scattered across notebooks and memory
- [ ] Customers know if/how their contact info will be used for marketing, and can opt out
- [ ] Payment is handled through a real processor, not stored manually

## Experience
- [ ] You've walked through customer-journey.md and know your weakest stage
- [ ] There's a simple way to ask for reviews after a good visit

## Growth readiness
- [ ] You know what would break first if bookings doubled (staffing, space, or the booking system)
- [ ] You've picked at least one tool from tools-and-integrations.md if you're still managing bookings by phone/memory

## Note

This business usually doesn't need the heavier compliance review that other packs (like real estate or auto body) require, unless you're handling something more sensitive like health-related services or gift card/payment storage beyond a standard processor. If in doubt, a quick read of `resources.md` in `framework/business-lifecycle/05-compliance-catch-up/` covers the basics.
