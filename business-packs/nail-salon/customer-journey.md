# Nail Salon — Customer Journey

## 1. Discovery
Customer finds you through Instagram, Google search, or a friend's recommendation. Photos of your work matter enormously here — this is a visual business.

## 2. Booking
They want it to be fast and easy — ideally without a phone call during business hours when they're at work. A booking link they can use anytime beats "call to book."

## 3. Pre-appointment
A confirmation and reminder (text or email) dramatically reduces no-shows. Customers appreciate not having to remember on their own.

## 4. The appointment
This is where the actual service quality lives — outside the scope of this system, but worth noting: a smooth check-in process sets the tone before the service even starts.

## 5. Checkout
Fast, simple payment. This is also the natural moment to mention a loyalty program or ask about booking their next appointment before they leave.

## 6. After the visit
A follow-up (even a simple "hope you're loving your nails!" text a few days later) plus a review request. This is where most salons leave value on the table — they do great work and never ask for the review that would bring in the next customer.

## Where to focus first

If you can only fix one stage, fix **stage 3 (pre-appointment reminders)**. It's the cheapest, most automatable fix, and directly reduces the revenue lost to no-shows.
