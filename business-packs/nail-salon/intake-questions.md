# Nail Salon — Intake Questions

This pack already has example questions in README.md — these go deeper for someone actually setting up their system.

## Understanding the business today

1. How do customers book appointments right now — phone, walk-in, an app, social media DM?
2. What happens if someone no-shows? Is there a policy, or does it just get absorbed?
3. Do you track customer preferences (favorite color, allergies, past services) anywhere, or is it all in a stylist's memory?

## Staff and scheduling

4. How many staff work here, and how is their schedule managed?
5. What happens when a staff member is out — does the system handle rebooking smoothly?

## Customer experience

6. What's the thing customers compliment you on most?
7. What's the thing that causes the most scheduling headaches?
8. Do you send any reminders before appointments? How far in advance?

## Money and promotions

9. How do customers pay — card, cash, app?
10. Do you run any loyalty programs, and if so, how is it tracked?
11. Do you collect contact info for marketing, and do customers know that's what it's for?

## Growth

12. If you got twice as many bookings tomorrow, what would break first — staffing, space, or the booking system itself?
