# Dental Office — Review Checklist

Work through this before you consider the practice's operations "launched" or fully modernized, whether newly opening or formalizing how an existing practice runs.

## Licensing and credentials
- [ ] Every provider's dental license is current and easy to verify if asked
- [ ] Any required state or local business licensing for the practice itself is in place
- [ ] Staff certifications (dental hygienists, assistants where licensure applies) are current

## HIPAA and patient data
- [ ] A HIPAA risk assessment has been done, and you know roughly when the next one should happen
- [ ] Access to patient records is limited by role, not open to every staff member by default
- [ ] Any software storing or transmitting patient data has a signed Business Associate Agreement (BAA) with the vendor
- [ ] Staff have been trained on basic HIPAA do's and don'ts (what can and can't be discussed, texted, or left on a voicemail)

## Patient scheduling and recall
- [ ] There's a system actively tracking which patients are due or overdue for recall visits (not just relying on patients to remember)
- [ ] Appointment reminders go out automatically, not manually per patient
- [ ] There's a documented no-show/late-cancellation policy

## Insurance and billing
- [ ] Insurance eligibility is verified before the appointment, not discovered at checkout
- [ ] Billing statements clearly explain what insurance covered and what the patient owes
- [ ] There's a process for handling a billing dispute or confused patient

## Infection control
- [ ] Sterilization protocols are documented and followed consistently
- [ ] Any required infection-control certifications or training are current

## Before you call it done
- [ ] You've read compliance-guidance.md in this folder
- [ ] You've picked at least one recall/reminder tool from tools-and-integrations.md if you're still relying on manual tracking
- [ ] You've walked through customer-journey.md and identified your weakest stage (recall is the most common one)
