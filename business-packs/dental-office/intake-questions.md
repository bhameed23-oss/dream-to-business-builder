# Dental Office — Intake Questions

Use these questions when a dental practice owner or office manager is first setting up their business system. Ask them in plain conversation, not as a form dump — the goal is understanding, not paperwork.

## Understanding the practice today

1. Is this a solo practice, a small group, or a larger multi-provider office?
2. General dentistry, a specialty (ortho, oral surgery, pediatric), or a mix?
3. What practice management software do you currently use, if any (Dentrix, Eaglesoft, Open Dental, or something else)?
4. How do patients currently book appointments — phone only, online, both?
5. How is the patient recall schedule (6-month cleanings, etc.) currently tracked?

## Patient experience

6. What's your current no-show/late-cancellation rate, roughly?
7. How do you remind patients about upcoming appointments right now?
8. What percentage of patients actually come back on schedule for their recall visit, roughly?
9. What's the biggest source of patient anxiety or complaints you hear?

## Insurance and billing

10. What percentage of patients have dental insurance versus paying out of pocket?
11. How is insurance eligibility verified — before the appointment, or discovered at check-in?
12. How are patient billing statements handled, and how often are they confusing to patients?

## Data and compliance

13. How is patient health information stored — a specific practice management system, paper charts, both?
14. Who on staff has access to patient records, and is that access role-based or does everyone see everything?
15. Has the practice had a HIPAA risk assessment done, and if so, when was the last one?
16. Are all providers' licenses current and easy to verify if asked?

## Operations

17. How many chairs/operatories does the office have, and how many are typically in use at once?
18. What's the biggest bottleneck in a typical day — scheduling, checkout, insurance calls, something else?
19. How is new-patient paperwork handled — on a clipboard at check-in, or in advance?

## Goals

20. If you could fix one thing about how the office runs day-to-day, what would it be?
21. Where do you want the practice to be in a year — same size and steady, more patients, another provider, another location?
