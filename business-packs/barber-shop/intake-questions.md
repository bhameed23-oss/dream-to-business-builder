# Barber Shop — Intake Questions

Use these questions when a barber or shop owner is first setting up their business. Ask them in plain conversation, not as a form dump — the goal is understanding, not paperwork.

## Understanding the business today

1. Is this a single-barber shop, or will multiple barbers work here?
2. If multiple barbers: are they employees, or are they renting a chair from you as independent barbers?
3. How do clients currently book — walk-in only, text, phone call, Instagram DM, an app?
4. What's a typical day look like right now — fully booked, mostly walk-in, a mix?
5. Do you keep any record of what a regular client usually gets, or is it all memory?

## Licensing and setup

6. Do you (and every barber who'll cut hair here) already hold a current barber license in this state?
7. Has the shop itself been through a health/sanitation inspection, or is that still ahead of you?
8. If you're renting chairs to other barbers, is there a written agreement in place yet?

## Volume and capacity

9. How many chairs/stations does the shop have?
10. On your busiest day, how many clients realistically get seen?
11. What's the average time per cut/service, and does that vary a lot by barber?

## Client experience

12. What do clients say keeps them coming back to you specifically?
13. Do you have a no-show or late-cancellation policy right now? Is it enforced?
14. How do first-time clients find out what to expect (pricing, what you specialize in, how to book)?

## Money

15. How do clients pay — cash, card, app (Venmo/CashApp), or a mix?
16. How is tipping handled?
17. If it's a booth-rental shop: how is rent collected, and what happens if someone's late paying it?

## Data and risk

18. Do you store client contact info or notes anywhere digital (an app, a spreadsheet, a phone's contacts)?
19. Does the shop carry liability insurance?
20. Have there ever been any incidents (allergic reaction, injury, client complaint) worth knowing about?

## Goals

21. If you could fix one thing about how the shop runs day-to-day, what would it be?
22. Where do you want the shop to be in a year — same size and steady, or growing to more chairs/locations?
