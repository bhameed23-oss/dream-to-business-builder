# Barber Shop — Review Checklist

Work through this before you consider the shop's setup "launched," whether you're opening a brand new shop or formalizing how an existing one runs.

## Licensing and legal basics
- [ ] The shop itself holds any required local business license
- [ ] Every barber cutting hair in the shop holds a current, valid state barber license
- [ ] If renting chairs to independent barbers, there's a written booth-rental agreement in place (not a handshake deal)
- [ ] Worker classification is clear (employee vs. independent contractor) for anyone working in the shop

## Health and sanitation
- [ ] The shop has passed (or is scheduled for) any required local health/sanitation inspection
- [ ] There's a clear routine for sanitizing tools and stations between clients
- [ ] Sanitation supplies are actually stocked, not "we'll get some"

## Booking and scheduling
- [ ] Clients have at least one easy way to book that doesn't require a phone call during business hours
- [ ] There's a written no-show/late-cancellation policy, and clients actually see it before booking
- [ ] Rebooking is either offered at checkout or automated with a reminder afterward

## Money
- [ ] Payments go through a real payment processor, not manually keyed/stored card numbers
- [ ] Tipping is built into the checkout flow rather than left to an awkward cash moment
- [ ] If it's a booth-rental shop, rent collection has a clear process and schedule

## Records and data
- [ ] Client contact info and preferences live in one place, not scattered across notebooks and someone's personal phone
- [ ] Access to client records is limited to people who need it
- [ ] The shop carries liability insurance

## Before you call it done
- [ ] You've read compliance-guidance.md in this folder
- [ ] You've picked at least one booking tool from tools-and-integrations.md if you're still relying on texts/memory
- [ ] You've walked through customer-journey.md and identified your weakest stage (rebooking is the most common one)
