# Construction — Tools and Integrations

Real tools, roughly ordered from simpler/lower-cost to full-featured construction management platforms. Start at the top — don't buy an enterprise construction ERP before you've outgrown a spreadsheet and shared calendar.

## Estimating and project management (the core system)

- **Buildertrend** — Paid, widely used by residential contractors and remodelers, handles estimating, scheduling, client communication, and change orders in one system.
- **Jobber** — Paid, popular with smaller trade businesses (electrical, plumbing, HVAC, general handyman work), handles scheduling, estimating, and invoicing.
- **Procore** — Paid, more geared toward commercial construction and larger projects, with deep project management and document control features.

## Estimating specifically

- **STACK** or **PlanSwift** — Paid takeoff/estimating software that helps calculate material quantities directly from plans, reducing the "the bid didn't match reality" problem.

## Scheduling and crew coordination

- Most of the platforms above (Buildertrend, Jobber, Procore) include scheduling features for coordinating crews and subcontractors across multiple job sites.

## Payment and progress billing

- **QuickBooks** (often integrated with the platforms above) — Widely used for invoicing, progress billing tied to project milestones, and general bookkeeping.
- Built-in progress-payment features in Buildertrend/Jobber/Procore reduce the manual work of tracking what's been billed at each project stage.

## Client communication

- Client portals built into Buildertrend and similar platforms centralize photos, updates, and change orders in one place the client can check, reducing the "did you get my text" back-and-forth.

## Reviews and reputation

- **Google Business Profile** (free) — Set this up first, with real project photos. A strong local portfolio and reviews are one of the biggest trust factors for a decision this large.

## Safety training and resources

- **OSHA 10/OSHA 30 training** — Widely recognized construction safety training courses; many general contractors require subcontractors to have completed one before working on a jobsite.

## Security and record-keeping basics

Because contracts, payment schedules, and client information are involved:
- Keep signed contracts and change orders in one accessible, backed-up place
- Use a real payment processor rather than manually keying and storing card numbers
