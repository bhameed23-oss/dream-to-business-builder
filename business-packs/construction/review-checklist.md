# Construction — Review Checklist

Work through this before you consider the business's operations "launched" or fully modernized, whether newly starting out or formalizing how an existing business runs.

## Licensing and legal basics
- [ ] The business holds all required contractor licenses for the type of work performed and the jurisdictions it works in
- [ ] Insurance (general liability and workers' compensation) is current and covers the actual scope of work
- [ ] Worker classification (employee vs. subcontractor) matches how the work actually happens

## Contracts and estimating
- [ ] There's a written contract template covering scope, payment schedule, and how change orders are handled
- [ ] Estimates reliably reflect real material and labor costs, not rough guesses
- [ ] Change orders are documented and signed, not verbal agreements

## Scheduling and permits
- [ ] There's a system coordinating crews/subcontractors across job sites, not relying on memory or a single phone
- [ ] Permit and inspection requirements are tracked and built into the project timeline
- [ ] Material ordering accounts for lead times so delays don't derail the schedule

## Safety
- [ ] There's a documented jobsite safety process
- [ ] Crew members and subcontractors have appropriate safety training for the work performed
- [ ] Any required safety equipment and signage is actually in use, not just available

## Money
- [ ] Progress payments are tied to clear, agreed-upon project milestones
- [ ] There's a documented process for following up on late payments, including lien rights where applicable
- [ ] Final invoices clearly match the signed contract plus any documented change orders

## Before you call it done
- [ ] You've read compliance-guidance.md in this folder
- [ ] You've picked at least one estimating/scheduling tool from tools-and-integrations.md if you're still tracking things manually
- [ ] You've walked through customer-journey.md and identified your weakest stage (proactive communication during the project is the most common one)
