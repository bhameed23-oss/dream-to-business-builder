# Construction — Intake Questions

Use these questions when a contractor or business owner is first setting up their business system. Ask them in plain conversation, not as a form dump — the goal is understanding, not paperwork.

## Understanding the business today

1. General contractor, or a specialty trade (electrical, plumbing, roofing, HVAC, etc.)?
2. Residential, commercial, or both?
3. Is it just you, a small crew, or do you also use subcontractors?
4. How do you currently estimate a job — a formula, experience-based guessing, formal takeoff software?
5. What project management or scheduling tool do you use, if any?

## Licensing and setup

6. Do you hold the required contractor license(s) for the work you do and the states/localities you work in?
7. Are your insurance policies (general liability, workers' comp) current?
8. If you use subcontractors, are they set up as genuine independent contractors, or does it look more like employment?

## Estimating and bidding

9. How often does an actual job cost come in higher than the bid, and why does that usually happen?
10. How do you handle change orders when a client wants something added mid-project?
11. What percentage margin are you typically targeting, and do you know if you're actually hitting it?

## Scheduling and materials

12. How do you coordinate scheduling across multiple job sites or crews?
13. How often do material delays or shortages throw off a project timeline?
14. How are permits and inspections tracked and scheduled?

## Client experience

15. How do you keep clients updated during a longer project?
16. What's the most common source of client frustration or dispute?
17. How do payment schedules typically work — deposit, progress payments, final payment?

## Money

18. How often do you have to chase down a late payment?
19. Do you use lien rights (mechanic's liens) when payment is seriously delayed?

## Safety and risk
20. Is there a documented jobsite safety process, or is it more informal?
21. Has the business had any safety incidents or OSHA-related issues?

## Goals

22. If you could fix one thing about how the business runs day-to-day, what would it be?
23. Where do you want the business to be in a year — same size and steady, bigger crew, more project volume?
