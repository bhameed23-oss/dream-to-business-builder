# Enterprise — Review Checklist

Work through this to assess whether the business's systems have actually kept pace with its size, not just its revenue.

## Ownership and decision-making
- [ ] Every major function (sales, operations, finance, HR, etc.) has one clear owner, not shared or unclear ownership
- [ ] There's a documented list of decisions the founder/owner no longer needs to personally make
- [ ] Decisions that fall outside anyone's clear ownership have a defined path to resolution

## Process and knowledge
- [ ] Key processes are documented somewhere the whole team can access, not just known by one person
- [ ] New hires are onboarded with a standardized process, not an ad hoc one that varies by who trains them
- [ ] If a key manager left tomorrow, there's a real answer for what would happen (not "it would be bad")

## Visibility and reporting
- [ ] Leadership has a real, current view of how the whole business is doing, not just their own department
- [ ] Reporting happens on a regular cadence, not only when something goes wrong
- [ ] Data from different departments/systems can actually be compared, not siloed and incompatible

## HR and people
- [ ] HR policies meet legal requirements for the current employee count (many requirements scale with headcount)
- [ ] Onboarding is standardized across departments and locations
- [ ] There's a clear escalation path for HR issues that doesn't depend entirely on the founder

## Risk and access
- [ ] Data access is reviewed periodically, not just granted once and forgotten
- [ ] Onboarding/offboarding revokes access promptly when someone leaves
- [ ] Insurance and vendor contracts have been reviewed at the business's current scale, not just when it was smaller

## Before you call it done
- [ ] You've read compliance-guidance.md in this folder
- [ ] You've picked at least one visibility/reporting tool from tools-and-integrations.md if leadership still lacks a whole-business view
- [ ] You've walked through customer-journey.md (the internal decision journey) and identified where ownership is unclear
