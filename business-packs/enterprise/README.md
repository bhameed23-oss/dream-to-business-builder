# Enterprise Business Pack

Unlike the other packs in this directory, this one isn't a single industry — it's for a business of *any* industry that has grown past the small-business stage into a larger, more complex organization (multiple departments, multiple locations, a real management layer between the owner and frontline staff). Use this pack alongside an industry-specific pack, not instead of it.

## Business summary

An enterprise-scale business has outgrown the point where one owner can personally oversee every decision. It typically has multiple departments or functions (sales, operations, finance, HR, IT), multiple locations or business units, and formal processes that replace what used to be informal, tribal knowledge. This is the "Scaling" and "Auditing" territory of the business lifecycle — the growth has outpaced the systems.

## Typical goals

- create clear ownership and decision rights so not everything routes through one person
- formalize processes that used to live only in someone's head
- build real reporting/visibility across departments and locations
- manage a larger workforce with consistent HR practices
- protect the business with proper insurance, contracts, and compliance at scale
- keep company culture intact as headcount grows past the size where everyone knows everyone

## Common pain points

- the owner or founder is still a bottleneck for decisions that should be delegated
- different departments or locations operate inconsistently, with no shared playbook
- data and reporting live in disconnected systems, making it hard to see the whole business clearly
- hiring has outpaced onboarding, training, and HR infrastructure
- risk exposure has grown (more employees, more locations, more contracts) without compliance keeping pace
- what worked at a smaller size (informal communication, ad hoc processes) breaks down at this size

## Important review areas

This business often touches:
- HR compliance (employment law scales with headcount — more requirements kick in at certain thresholds)
- data security across more systems and more people with access
- contract and vendor management at a larger scale
- financial controls and reporting across departments/locations
- leadership structure and delegation of decision-making

Before scaling further, review:
- HR policies and whether they meet legal requirements at the current headcount
- data access controls across a growing number of systems and employees
- financial reporting and whether leadership actually has visibility into the whole business
- decision-making structure and whether the owner/founder is still an unnecessary bottleneck

## Example plan

1. map out current departments/functions and identify where ownership is unclear
2. formalize the processes that currently only exist in one person's head
3. build a reporting structure that gives leadership real visibility across the business
4. review HR policies against legal requirements for the current employee count
5. review data access and security across the growing number of systems in use
6. build a delegation plan so the founder isn't the bottleneck for routine decisions
7. review vendor contracts and insurance coverage at the current scale
8. set a regular cadence (monthly/quarterly) for leadership to review the whole business together

## Example automation opportunities

- centralized dashboards pulling data from multiple departments/systems into one view
- standardized onboarding workflows for new hires across departments
- automated approval workflows that don't require the owner personally for every decision
- automated compliance/policy-acknowledgment tracking for a larger workforce
- consolidated reporting cadence (automated report generation for leadership meetings)

## Example design direction

This pack is less about a public-facing website and more about internal systems — but if there's a public presence, it should reflect the business's actual current scale (multiple locations, established credibility) rather than looking like a smaller operation than it is.

## Example AI use cases

- draft a delegation plan identifying which decisions the founder should stop personally handling
- write a standardized onboarding checklist for a new department or location
- summarize disparate departmental reports into one leadership-level summary
- draft an internal process document turning "how Sarah does it" into a repeatable playbook
- help identify where informal processes are creating risk as headcount grows
