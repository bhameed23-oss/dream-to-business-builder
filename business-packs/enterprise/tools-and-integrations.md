# Enterprise — Tools and Integrations

Unlike the industry packs, this isn't one core system — it's about connecting and standardizing the systems a growing business has likely already accumulated one at a time.

## Company-wide visibility and reporting

- **A BI (business intelligence) tool like Looker Studio (free)** — Free, connects to spreadsheets and many common business tools to build a single dashboard leadership can actually check, instead of assembling numbers manually before every meeting.
- **Power BI** or **Tableau** — Paid, more robust options once the business has outgrown what a free tool can pull together, especially with data spread across multiple systems.

## Project and process management

- **Asana**, **Monday.com**, or **ClickUp** — Paid, widely used for standardizing how work and processes are tracked across departments, replacing the "ask around to find out what's going on" problem.
- Documented, repeatable processes (Standard Operating Procedures) matter more than which specific tool holds them — the goal is getting tribal knowledge out of individual heads and into something the whole team can reference.

## HR and people systems

- **Gusto** or **Rippling** — Paid, handle payroll, benefits, and HR compliance in one system, which becomes increasingly important as employee count crosses thresholds that trigger new legal requirements (see compliance-guidance.md).
- **BambooHR** — Paid, focused specifically on HR record-keeping, onboarding, and performance management at a scale beyond what a spreadsheet can handle.

## Access and data security

- **A password manager with team/business features (like 1Password Business)** — Manages shared access across a growing number of systems and employees, with the ability to revoke access quickly when someone leaves.
- **Single sign-on (SSO) tools like Okta** — Once the number of systems and employees grows large enough, SSO centralizes access control instead of managing logins system-by-system.

## Communication and knowledge management

- **Slack** or **Microsoft Teams** — Widely used for cross-department communication at scale, replacing informal hallway conversations that don't scale past a certain size.
- **Notion** or **Confluence** — Widely used to document processes and decisions so they don't live only in one person's memory.

## Security and record-keeping basics

Because a growing business accumulates more systems and more people with access over time:
- Periodically review who has access to what, and remove access that's no longer needed
- Standardize onboarding/offboarding so access is granted and revoked consistently
- See the compliance-guidance.md file in this folder for HR and legal considerations that scale with headcount
