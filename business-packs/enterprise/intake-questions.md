# Enterprise — Intake Questions

Use these questions when a growing business's leadership is assessing whether their systems have kept pace with their size. Ask them in plain conversation, not as a form dump — the goal is understanding, not paperwork.

## Understanding the business today

1. Roughly how many employees does the business have now, and how many departments or functions exist?
2. How many locations or business units, if more than one?
3. What's an example of a decision that still routes through you personally that probably shouldn't?
4. What's an example of a process that only one person really knows how to do?
5. How does leadership currently get visibility into how the whole business is doing (a meeting, a dashboard, informal check-ins)?

## Structure and delegation

6. Who owns what, clearly? Are there areas where two people think they own the same thing, or no one does?
7. If a key manager left tomorrow, how much would break?
8. How are decisions made when something falls outside anyone's clear ownership?

## Systems and data

9. How many different software systems does the business use across departments, roughly?
10. Do those systems talk to each other, or does someone manually move data between them?
11. Who has access to what data, and is that access actually reviewed, or does it just accumulate over time?

## People and HR

12. Does HR policy and practice actually reflect legal requirements for your current headcount? (Many requirements kick in at specific employee-count thresholds.)
13. How standardized is onboarding across different departments or locations?
14. What's the biggest people-related risk you're aware of right now?

## Risk and compliance

15. Has anyone reviewed insurance coverage and contracts at the business's current size (versus when it was smaller)?
16. Is there a written policy on data security and who's responsible for it?
17. What's the biggest thing that keeps you up at night about how the business runs today?

## Goals

18. If you could fix one structural thing about how the business runs, what would it be?
19. Where do you want the business to be in a year or two — more locations, more departments, or focused on strengthening what already exists?
