# Nonprofit — Tools and Integrations

Real tools, roughly ordered from free/low-cost to full-featured donor management platforms. Many vendors offer nonprofit discounts or free tiers — always ask directly, since this is standard in the sector and not always advertised prominently.

## Donor management / CRM (the core system)

- **Little Green Light** — Paid but affordable, purpose-built donor CRM for small-to-mid-size nonprofits, tracks gifts, communication history, and recurring donors.
- **Bloomerang** — Paid, donor CRM with a strong focus on donor retention reporting, popular with small-to-mid-size organizations.
- **Salesforce Nonprofit Cloud** — More complex, but Salesforce offers a well-known nonprofit donation/licensing program worth researching directly with Salesforce if the organization is larger or growing quickly.

## Online giving and payment processing

- **Donorbox** or **Give Lively** — Widely used, straightforward online donation page builders with recurring-donation support, often lower-fee or nonprofit-friendly pricing compared to general payment processors.
- **PayPal Giving Fund** — A no-fee donation option for eligible 501(c)(3) organizations, worth setting up alongside a primary donation page.

## Volunteer management

- **SignUpGenius** — Free/low-cost, simple volunteer shift scheduling, widely used by smaller organizations.
- **Volunteer Hub** or built-in volunteer modules in some donor CRMs — More robust options once volunteer coordination outgrows a simple sign-up sheet.

## Grant tracking

- A dedicated spreadsheet or the grant-tracking features in tools like Bloomerang can help track application deadlines, reporting requirements, and restricted-fund usage — critical since missing a grant report deadline can jeopardize future funding.

## Nonprofit-specific software discounts

- **TechSoup** — A well-known nonprofit technology marketplace offering discounted or donated software licenses (including Microsoft, Adobe, and others) specifically for eligible nonprofits. Worth checking before paying full price for common software.

## Reviews and trust signals

- **Charity Navigator** and **GuideStar/Candid** — Widely recognized nonprofit rating and transparency platforms. Having a complete, up-to-date profile on these builds donor trust, especially for larger or first-time gifts.

## Security and record-keeping basics

Because donor payment information and beneficiary data may be involved:
- Use a real payment processor for online giving rather than manually handling card numbers
- Limit access to donor and beneficiary records to staff who need it
