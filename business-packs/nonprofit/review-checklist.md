# Nonprofit — Review Checklist

Work through this before you consider the organization's systems "launched" or fully modernized, whether newly forming or formalizing how an existing organization runs.

## Tax-exempt status and filings
- [ ] 501(c)(3) (or other applicable) tax-exempt status is confirmed current and in good standing
- [ ] Form 990 (or 990-EZ/990-N, depending on size) has been filed on time in recent years
- [ ] The organization is registered to solicit donations in every state where it actively fundraises, if applicable

## Governance
- [ ] Bylaws exist, are followed, and are reasonably current
- [ ] Board meetings happen on a regular, documented cadence with minutes kept
- [ ] A conflict-of-interest policy exists and board members have actually signed it

## Funding
- [ ] Funding isn't dangerously dependent on a single grant or donor
- [ ] Restricted grant funds are tracked separately from general operating funds
- [ ] There's a real donor management system, not just a spreadsheet or memory

## Donor relationships
- [ ] Donors receive a timely, genuine thank-you after giving
- [ ] There's an ongoing communication plan (updates, impact stories) beyond just asking for the next gift
- [ ] Recurring/monthly donors are tracked and nurtured distinctly from one-time givers

## Programs and volunteers
- [ ] There's a clear, simple way to describe and measure program impact
- [ ] Volunteers are recruited, scheduled, and supported with a real process
- [ ] If volunteers work with children, elderly people, or other vulnerable populations, background checks are in place

## Money and oversight
- [ ] Financial oversight is clearly assigned (treasurer, finance committee, bookkeeper)
- [ ] The organization has had a financial review or audit appropriate to its size and funding sources
- [ ] Access to financial and donor systems is limited to those who need it

## Before you call it done
- [ ] You've read compliance-guidance.md in this folder
- [ ] You've picked at least one donor management tool from tools-and-integrations.md if you're still tracking gifts manually
- [ ] You've walked through customer-journey.md and identified your weakest stage (staying connected with existing donors is the most common one)
