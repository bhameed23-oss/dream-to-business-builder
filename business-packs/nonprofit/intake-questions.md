# Nonprofit — Intake Questions

Use these questions when a nonprofit's leader or staff member is first setting up the organization's systems. Ask them in plain conversation, not as a form dump — the goal is understanding, not paperwork.

## Understanding the organization today

1. What's the mission, in a sentence or two, and who does the organization actually serve?
2. What's the organization's tax-exempt status (501(c)(3) or another type), and is it currently in good standing?
3. What does the funding mix look like — grants, individual donors, events, earned revenue, a mix?
4. How many paid staff versus volunteers keep things running?
5. What donor/CRM system do you use, if any (a spreadsheet counts as an honest answer)?

## Funding and donors

6. Roughly what percentage of funding comes from your single largest source? (If it's a large percentage, that's a real risk worth flagging.)
7. How do you currently track and thank donors after a gift?
8. Do you have recurring/monthly donors, and how are they managed differently from one-time givers?
9. If you receive grants, how do you track restricted funds separately from general operating funds?

## Programs and impact

10. How do you currently measure or describe your impact?
11. What does a typical program cycle or service delivery look like?
12. What's the biggest operational bottleneck in delivering the program?

## Volunteers and staff

13. How are volunteers recruited, scheduled, and managed?
14. If volunteers work with children, elderly people, or other vulnerable populations, is there a background-check process?
15. How engaged is the board, honestly — do they show up and help, or mostly just exist on paper?

## Compliance

16. When is your Form 990 (or 990-EZ/990-N) due, and has it always been filed on time?
17. Is the organization registered to solicit donations in every state where it actively fundraises, if that applies?
18. How is board governance documented — bylaws, meeting minutes, conflict-of-interest policy?

## Money

19. How is financial oversight handled — a treasurer, a finance committee, an external bookkeeper/accountant?
20. Has the organization ever had an audit or financial review, and when?

## Goals

21. If you could fix one thing about how the organization runs day-to-day, what would it be?
22. Where do you want the organization to be in a year — same size and stable, more programs, more funding diversity?
