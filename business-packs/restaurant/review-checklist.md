# Restaurant — Review Checklist

Work through this before you consider the restaurant's operations "launched," whether opening new or formalizing how an existing place runs.

## Food safety
- [ ] Staff who handle food hold current food handler certification (or your state's equivalent)
- [ ] There's a routine, logged process for checking food temperatures
- [ ] The last health inspection result is known and any issues from it have been addressed

## Licensing
- [ ] The restaurant holds any required local food service license
- [ ] If serving alcohol, the liquor license is current and staff have completed any required server training
- [ ] Any required signage (health permit, choking-hazard notices, etc.) is posted where required

## Operations
- [ ] There's one clear POS system, or a clear process for reconciling orders across multiple sources (in-house, phone, delivery apps)
- [ ] Inventory is tracked well enough that spoilage/waste is a known, managed number, not a surprise
- [ ] There's a documented process (checklist) for opening and closing shifts

## Staffing and labor law
- [ ] Scheduling accounts for required breaks and overtime rules
- [ ] If employing anyone under 18, their work hours follow your state's minor labor laws
- [ ] Tip pooling/reporting follows a documented, consistent process

## Money
- [ ] Payments are processed through a real payment system, not manually keyed/stored
- [ ] Third-party delivery app fees are known and factored into menu pricing
- [ ] There's visibility into daily/weekly cash flow, not just end-of-month totals

## Before you call it done
- [ ] You've read compliance-guidance.md in this folder
- [ ] You've picked at least one POS/inventory tool from tools-and-integrations.md if you're still tracking things on paper
- [ ] You've walked through customer-journey.md and identified your weakest stage (consistency during "the meal" is the most common one)
