# Restaurant — Intake Questions

Use these questions when a restaurant owner is first setting up their business system. Ask them in plain conversation, not as a form dump — the goal is understanding, not paperwork.

## Understanding the business today

1. What kind of place is this — dine-in, takeout-focused, food truck, a mix?
2. Do you serve alcohol? If so, is your liquor license and server training current?
3. What POS (point of sale) system do you use right now, if any?
4. How do orders come in — in person, phone, your own online ordering, third-party apps (DoorDash, Uber Eats, etc.), or several of these?
5. How do those different order sources get reconciled — do you have one clear picture of the day's total sales?

## Food safety and operations

6. Are staff who handle food certified (food handler card, ServSafe, or your state's equivalent)?
7. Is there a routine for checking and logging food temperatures?
8. How's your relationship with your local health inspector — smooth, or has there been a rough inspection recently?
9. How do you track inventory, and how often does something go bad before it's used?

## Staffing

10. How many staff work a typical shift, split between kitchen and front-of-house?
11. What's your biggest staffing headache — no-shows, turnover, finding people at all?
12. How is scheduling currently done (paper, group text, an app)?
13. If you employ anyone under 18, do you know your state's rules on their work hours?

## Customer experience

14. What brings customers back versus a one-time visit?
15. Do you have any kind of loyalty or repeat-customer program?
16. What's the most common complaint you hear?

## Money

17. Roughly what percentage of revenue goes to third-party delivery app fees, if you use them?
18. How is tipping handled — pooled, individual, does it get reported properly?
19. How tight is cash flow, especially early in the week versus weekends?

## Data and risk

20. Do you store customer info (loyalty program, online ordering accounts) anywhere digital?
21. Who has access to sales and financial data?

## Goals

22. If you could fix one thing about how the restaurant runs day-to-day, what would it be?
23. Where do you want the business to be in a year — same size and steady, more locations, or a different format (like adding delivery-only hours)?
