# Cleaning Service — Tools and Integrations

Real tools, roughly ordered from "free and simple" to "paid and full-featured." Start at the top — don't buy a full field-service platform before you've outgrown a shared calendar and a spreadsheet.

## Scheduling and recurring billing (the core system)

- **A shared Google Calendar + Google Sheet** — Free. Fine for a solo cleaner or very small operation just starting out.
- **Housecall Pro** — Paid, built for home-service businesses including cleaning. Handles scheduling, recurring billing, dispatch, and customer communication in one place.
- **Jobber** — Paid alternative, also popular with home-service and cleaning businesses, similar feature set (scheduling, invoicing, client management).
- **ZenMaid** — Paid, built specifically for cleaning businesses (as opposed to home services generally), with recurring scheduling and crew management as a core focus.

## Payment and recurring billing

- Most of the above (Housecall Pro, Jobber, ZenMaid) include built-in recurring billing, which is the single most useful feature for a business built on repeat visits — worth prioritizing when comparing tools.
- **Square** — A simpler standalone option if you're not ready for a full field-service platform yet but want reliable card payments and basic recurring invoices.

## Route and crew management

- The field-service tools above (Housecall Pro, Jobber, ZenMaid) typically include route/dispatch views once you have more than one crew running jobs in a day.
- **Google Maps** — Free, fine for manually planning routes when it's just you or one crew.

## Reviews and reputation

- **Google Business Profile** (free) — Set this up first. "Bonded and insured [service] near me" is a real, common search, and a strong review base is one of the biggest trust factors in this industry.

## Background checks

- **Checkr** — A widely used background-check service for on-demand and service businesses; useful before sending anyone into a client's home, whether they're an employee or subcontractor.

## Security and record-keeping basics

Because access codes, keys, and client addresses are involved:
- Track who has which key/code and revoke access when someone leaves
- Use a password manager for shared logins
- See the compliance-guidance.md file in this folder before deciding how access information is stored
