# Cleaning Service — Review Checklist

Work through this before you consider the business's setup "launched," whether you're just starting out or formalizing how an existing service runs.

## Trust and access
- [ ] There's a consistent, tracked way client keys/codes are stored (not scattered across different people's phones or a drawer)
- [ ] Anyone entering a client's home or business — employee or subcontractor — has gone through a background check
- [ ] Access is revoked promptly when a cleaner or subcontractor stops working with you

## Quoting and booking
- [ ] There's a consistent quoting process, not "it depends who answers the phone"
- [ ] Clients have a clear way to book that doesn't require playing phone tag
- [ ] What's included in a standard cleaning (vs. an add-on, like inside the oven or fridge) is written down somewhere clients can see

## Quality and consistency
- [ ] There's a checklist or standard so different cleaners produce a similar result
- [ ] There's a clear process for handling a quality complaint
- [ ] Recurring clients' specific preferences/instructions are recorded somewhere, not just remembered

## Money
- [ ] Recurring clients are billed automatically, not chased down each visit
- [ ] One-off jobs have a fast, easy way to pay
- [ ] The business carries liability insurance and, ideally, bonding

## Staffing
- [ ] Worker classification (employee vs. independent contractor) matches how the work actually happens
- [ ] There's a real plan for what happens when someone calls out sick or no-shows

## Before you call it done
- [ ] You've read compliance-guidance.md in this folder
- [ ] You've picked at least one scheduling/billing tool from tools-and-integrations.md if you're still relying on texts/memory
- [ ] You've walked through customer-journey.md and identified your weakest stage (the first cleaning is the most common one)
