# Cleaning Service — Intake Questions

Use these questions when a cleaning business owner is first setting up their business system. Ask them in plain conversation, not as a form dump — the goal is understanding, not paperwork.

## Understanding the business today

1. Is this mostly residential, commercial, or a mix?
2. Mostly recurring clients (weekly/biweekly/monthly), one-off jobs, or a mix?
3. How do you currently quote a job — walkthrough, photos/video, or a phone description?
4. How do clients currently book — phone, text, a website form, word of mouth?
5. Is it just you, or do you have employees/subcontractors doing the cleaning?

## Access and trust

6. How do you currently get into a client's home or business when they're not there — key, lockbox, code, they let you in?
7. How are keys/codes tracked and stored right now?
8. Do you run background checks on anyone who goes into a client's property?

## Volume and capacity

9. How many jobs does a typical crew/cleaner handle in a day?
10. How is the day's route planned — is driving time between jobs a real problem?
11. What happens when someone calls out sick or a client reschedules last-minute?

## Client experience

12. What makes a client stick with you long-term versus canceling after a few visits?
13. Do you have a consistency process (checklist) so different cleaners produce the same result?
14. How do you handle a complaint about quality?

## Money

15. How do you bill recurring clients — automatically, or do you invoice each time?
16. How do you bill one-off jobs, and how often do you have to chase payment?
17. Do you carry liability insurance and bonding?

## Data and risk

18. Do you store client addresses, access codes, or payment info anywhere digital?
19. Who has access to that information?
20. If you use subcontractors, are they set up as actual independent contractors (own equipment, own schedule) or does it look more like employment?

## Goals

21. If you could fix one thing about how the business runs day-to-day, what would it be?
22. Where do you want the business to be in a year — same size and steady, more recurring clients, or growing to more crews?
