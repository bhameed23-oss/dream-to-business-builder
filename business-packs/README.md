# Business Pack Directory

This directory holds reusable, industry-specific business packs. Each pack gives someone starting or improving a business in that industry: the intake questions to ask, the customer journey to design around, real tools worth using, a launch-readiness checklist, and the compliance areas specific to that industry.

## Pack structure

Every pack in this directory follows the same six-file structure:

- **README.md** — business summary, typical goals, common pain points, important review areas, an example plan, automation opportunities, design direction, and AI use cases
- **intake-questions.md** — plain-conversation questions to understand the business before building anything
- **customer-journey.md** — step-by-step map of what a customer (or, for the enterprise pack, an internal decision) actually experiences, with a call-out for where to focus first
- **tools-and-integrations.md** — real, named tools for that industry, ordered from free/simple to paid/full-featured
- **review-checklist.md** — a launch-readiness checklist to work through before calling the setup "done"
- **compliance-guidance.md** — the specific legal/regulatory areas that industry needs to review, referencing only real, verifiable sources (a real government agency, a real named standard, a real licensing board) — never invented or generic legal advice

Every fact, tool, and regulation named across these packs is real and independently verifiable — nothing here is fabricated or guessed.
