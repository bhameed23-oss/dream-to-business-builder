# Legal Office — Review Checklist

Work through this before you consider the practice's operations "launched" or fully modernized, whether newly opening or formalizing how an existing firm runs.

## Licensing and ethics
- [ ] Every attorney's bar license is current and in good standing
- [ ] Advertising and marketing materials (including the website) comply with state bar advertising rules
- [ ] Fee agreements comply with state bar requirements for the type of fee arrangement used (contingency, flat, hourly)

## Conflicts and intake
- [ ] There's a systematic conflict-of-interest check before any new matter is accepted, not an informal memory check
- [ ] New client intake has a consistent process and reasonable response time
- [ ] Engagement letters clearly explain scope, fees, and what the client can expect

## Deadlines and case management
- [ ] Deadlines are tracked in a system with redundancy (not one person's calendar alone)
- [ ] There's a documented process for what happens if the responsible attorney/paralegal is unexpectedly out
- [ ] Case status is trackable without relying on one person's memory of where things stand

## Trust accounting (if applicable)
- [ ] Client trust funds (IOLTA) are kept separate from operating funds
- [ ] Trust account reconciliation happens on a regular, documented schedule
- [ ] Whoever handles trust accounting understands the state bar's specific rules for it

## Client communication
- [ ] Clients receive proactive status updates, not just responses when they ask
- [ ] Client communication is centralized somewhere the whole team (not just one person) can see it
- [ ] There's a documented process for handling an unhappy or confused client

## Money
- [ ] Time is tracked consistently enough that billable hours aren't being lost
- [ ] Invoices clearly explain what's being billed
- [ ] There's a reasonable process for following up on unpaid invoices

## Before you call it done
- [ ] You've read compliance-guidance.md in this folder
- [ ] You've picked at least one practice management tool from tools-and-integrations.md if you're still tracking things manually
- [ ] You've walked through customer-journey.md and identified your weakest stage (proactive communication during the matter is the most common one)
