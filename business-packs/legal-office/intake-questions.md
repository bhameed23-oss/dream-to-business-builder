# Legal Office — Intake Questions

Use these questions when an attorney or office manager is first setting up their business system. Ask them in plain conversation, not as a form dump — the goal is understanding, not paperwork.

## Understanding the practice today

1. Solo practice, small firm, or larger multi-attorney office?
2. What practice area(s) — family law, immigration, personal injury, estate planning, general practice, something else?
3. What practice management software do you use, if any (Clio, MyCase, PracticePanther, or something else)?
4. How do potential clients currently reach you — referrals, website form, phone, walk-in?
5. What does your intake process look like from first contact to signed engagement letter?

## Deadlines and case management

6. How are case deadlines tracked right now — a calendaring system, a paralegal's memory, something else?
7. Is there a backup/redundancy if the person who normally tracks deadlines is out sick?
8. Has the firm ever had a close call with a missed deadline?

## Client communication

9. How is client communication tracked — is it all in email, or centralized somewhere?
10. How quickly do clients typically hear back from you after reaching out?
11. What's the most common client complaint or frustration you hear?

## Billing and trust accounting

12. Do you bill hourly, flat fee, contingency, or a mix?
13. How is time tracked, and how confident are you that all billable time actually gets captured?
14. Do you handle client trust funds (IOLTA)? If so, how is that account reconciled?
15. How often do you have to chase down unpaid invoices?

## Compliance and risk

16. How do you check for conflicts of interest before taking a new matter?
17. Is client information (documents, communications) stored in a way that protects privilege and confidentiality?
18. Who has access to client files, and is that access limited by who's actually working the matter?

## Goals

19. If you could fix one thing about how the practice runs day-to-day, what would it be?
20. Where do you want the practice to be in a year — same size and steady, more clients, another attorney, a new practice area?
