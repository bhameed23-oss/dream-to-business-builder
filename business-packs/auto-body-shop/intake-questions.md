# Auto Body Shop — Intake Questions

Use these questions when a shop owner is first setting up their business system. Ask them in plain conversation, not as a form dump — the goal is understanding, not paperwork.

## Understanding the business today

1. How do customers currently reach you — phone, walk-in, website, referral?
2. When a car comes in, what's the very first thing that happens? Walk me through it.
3. How do you currently estimate repair cost and timeline?
4. Where do you keep customer and vehicle records right now (paper, spreadsheet, shop management software)?
5. How do customers find out the status of their repair — do they call you, or do you tell them?

## Volume and capacity

6. About how many vehicles are in the shop at once?
7. How many people work here, and who handles what (intake, repair, customer communication, billing)?
8. What's your busiest week ever looked like — what broke down?

## Customer experience

9. What do customers complain about most often?
10. What makes a customer choose you over another shop?
11. Do you send any reminders or follow-ups after a repair is done?

## Money and paperwork

12. How do customers pay you — card, check, insurance direct pay?
13. Do you deal with insurance companies directly, or does the customer handle that?
14. How do you handle a customer disputing an estimate or a charge?

## Data and risk

15. Do you store customer contact info, vehicle VINs, or insurance details anywhere digital?
16. Who has access to that information?
17. Have you ever had a mix-up with a customer's records or a lost document?

## Goals

18. If you could wave a wand and fix one thing about how the shop runs, what would it be?
19. Where do you want the business to be in a year?
