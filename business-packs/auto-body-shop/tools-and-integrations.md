# Auto Body Shop — Tools and Integrations

Real tools, roughly ordered from "free and simple" to "paid and full-featured." Start at the top — don't buy a full shop management system before you've outgrown a spreadsheet.

## Shop management (the core system)

- **A shared Google Sheet** — Free. Good enough for tracking vehicles, status, and contact info when you're small. Columns: customer name, phone, vehicle, status, estimate, promised date.
- **Shopmonkey** — Paid, built specifically for auto repair/body shops. Handles intake, estimates, status tracking, and customer communication in one place. Worth it once a spreadsheet becomes unmanageable.
- **Tekmetric** — Paid alternative to Shopmonkey, also auto-repair specific, popular with body shops that also want reporting.

## Customer communication

- **Text messaging via your shop management tool** (most of the above include this) — automates status updates without you calling each customer.
- **Google Voice** — Free business phone number if you don't have one yet, separate from your personal cell.

## Estimating

- **CCC ONE / Mitchell / Audatex** — Industry-standard collision estimating software, often required for insurance work. If you do insurance-direct repairs, you likely already know these; if not starting fresh, ask your insurance contacts which one they expect.

## Reviews and reputation

- **Google Business Profile** (free) — Set this up first, before anything else. It's how most customers will find and vet you.

## Security basics for this industry

Because you handle vehicle ownership info, insurance details, and sometimes payment info:
- Use a password manager for shared logins (not sticky notes on the monitor)
- Limit who has access to customer records to people who actually need it
- See the compliance-guidance.md file in this folder before storing any of this digitally
