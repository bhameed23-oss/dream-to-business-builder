# Auto Body Shop — Review Checklist

Work through this before you consider your customer intake and communication system "launched," whether you're just formalizing an existing shop or setting up a new one.

## Intake and estimates
- [ ] There's a consistent way every vehicle gets checked in (not "whoever's free writes it on a pad")
- [ ] Customers get a written estimate, not just a verbal number
- [ ] The estimate process explains what's OEM vs. aftermarket parts, if applicable in your state

## Communication
- [ ] Customers know how they'll be updated on repair status (text, call, portal — pick one and be consistent)
- [ ] There's a plan for what happens if a repair runs longer than promised
- [ ] Someone follows up after the car is picked up

## Records and data
- [ ] Customer and vehicle records live in one place, not scattered across notebooks, texts, and memory
- [ ] Access to customer records is limited to people who need it
- [ ] You know how long you're keeping records and whether that meets your state's requirements

## Money
- [ ] Payment is processed through a real payment system, not stored/keyed manually
- [ ] There's a clear process for handling a billing dispute

## Before you call it done
- [ ] You've read compliance-guidance.md in this folder
- [ ] You've picked at least one tool from tools-and-integrations.md if you're still using paper/memory
- [ ] You've walked through customer-journey.md and identified your weakest stage
