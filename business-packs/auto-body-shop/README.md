# Auto Body Shop Business Pack

This pack is for auto body shops, collision centers, repair facilities, and related vehicle service businesses.

## Business summary

An auto body shop repairs collision damage, completes cosmetic work, schedules appointments, communicates with customers, and manages paperwork for vehicle repairs.

## Typical goals

- manage repair appointments
- collect intake information from customers
- create job folders or records
- track parts, labor, and status updates
- keep customers informed without constant calls
- automate reminders and status communications
- improve trust and customer experience

## Common pain points

- customers call repeatedly for status updates
- repair intake is inconsistent
- vehicle information and documents are scattered
- communication is manual and misunderstood
- staff are overloaded with scheduling and follow-up
- missing records create rework and customer frustration

## Important review areas

This business often touches:
- customer personal information
- vehicle records and ownership details
- disability or insurance coordination
- payment and estimate tracking
- claim or policy information
- vehicle documentation

Before live launch, review:
- privacy and document handling
- secure storage and access
- customer communication consent
- payment process and security
- who can access customer repair records

## Example plan

1. define intake and estimate process
2. create a customer record system
3. set up repair status communication flow
4. automate reminder and update messages
5. build a vehicle job tracking workflow
6. review privacy and access rules
7. choose a secure booking or contact form
8. launch with a simple, trustworthy customer experience

## Example automation opportunities

- intake forms for repair estimates
- appointment scheduling and reminders
- status updates during repair progress
- estimate approval flows
- insurance claim document collection
- customer follow-up after repair completion
- review request automation
- staff task routing

## Example design direction

- clear and trustworthy customer communication
- visible repair process and timing information
- clean forms and appointment booking
- strong local credibility and professionalism
- simple service pages and policy details

## Example AI use cases

- draft customer status updates
- summarize vehicle repair notes
- build an estimate follow-up workflow
- route customer questions to the right person
- help create a professional FAQ or claims process
- flag missing customer information or documents
