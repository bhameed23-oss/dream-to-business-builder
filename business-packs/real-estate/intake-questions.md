# Real Estate — Intake Questions

Ask these when helping a real estate agent, team, or small brokerage set up their business system.

## Understanding the business today

1. What's your focus — buyers, sellers, rentals, property management, or a mix?
2. Where do your leads come from right now (referrals, Zillow, your own website, social media, open houses)?
3. Walk me through what happens the moment a new lead comes in, step by step.
4. Where do you keep track of leads and clients today — a CRM, a spreadsheet, your phone contacts?

## Lead handling

5. How fast do you typically respond to a new inquiry?
6. What happens to a lead if you're busy or on vacation?
7. How do you decide which leads are worth your time versus which ones are just browsing?

## Client experience

8. What does a client's experience look like from "interested" to "closed"?
9. What do clients say frustrates them most during a transaction?
10. How do you coordinate with lenders, inspectors, title companies, and other agents?

## Compliance and data

11. Do you collect and store any financial or mortgage-related documents from clients?
12. Are you aware of fair housing requirements in your area, and do you have a process to stay compliant in your marketing and client interactions?
13. Do you have a system for keeping required transaction documents and disclosures?

## Growth

14. If leads doubled tomorrow, what would break first?
15. What's the one thing that would make the biggest difference to your business this year?
