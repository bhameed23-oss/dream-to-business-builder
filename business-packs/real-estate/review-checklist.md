# Real Estate — Review Checklist

## Lead handling
- [ ] New leads get a response within minutes, not hours (speed-to-lead is the single biggest lever in this business)
- [ ] There's a backup plan for leads that come in when you're unavailable (auto-response, routing to a teammate, etc.)
- [ ] Leads are tracked in one place, not scattered across texts, emails, and memory

## Client experience
- [ ] Clients know what to expect at each stage of a transaction (you've walked through customer-journey.md)
- [ ] There's a consistent follow-up process after closing

## Compliance
- [ ] Listing descriptions and ad targeting have been checked against fair housing guidelines
- [ ] You know your state's required disclosures and retention periods for transaction files
- [ ] Client financial documents are stored securely and only shared with parties who need them

## Tools
- [ ] You've picked at least one tool from tools-and-integrations.md if you're still managing leads by memory or scattered notes
- [ ] Your transaction coordination tool (or your brokerage's) meets your state's document retention requirements

## Before you call it done
- [ ] You've read compliance-guidance.md in this folder
- [ ] You've identified your weakest stage in customer-journey.md and have a plan to address it
