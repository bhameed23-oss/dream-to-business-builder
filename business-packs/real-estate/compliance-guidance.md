# Real Estate — Compliance Guidance

**This is a starting point for understanding what to review, not legal advice.** Real estate is one of the more heavily regulated small-business categories — a licensed real estate attorney or your managing broker should be your first stop for anything specific.

## Fair housing

The Fair Housing Act prohibits discrimination in housing based on race, color, national origin, religion, sex, familial status, or disability. This affects:
- How you write listing descriptions (avoid language that implies a preference for or against any protected group)
- How you market properties (targeting ads in ways that exclude protected groups is a real legal risk)
- How you treat inquiries (consistent process for everyone, not different effort based on who's asking)

**[HUD - Fair Housing](https://www.hud.gov/program_offices/fair_housing_equal_opp)** — the federal resource on fair housing law.

## Licensing and disclosure

- You (or your brokerage) are licensed by your state's real estate commission — check that specific state for required disclosures (property condition, agency relationship, etc.)
- Search "[your state] real estate commission required disclosures" for the exact list.

## Data you're likely handling

- Client financial information (income, pre-approval letters)
- Property and title documents
- Signed contracts and disclosures

## Areas to review

1. **Document retention** — Your state's real estate commission almost certainly has a minimum retention period for transaction files. Check it.
2. **Client financial data** — If you're collecting pre-approval letters or financial documents, make sure they're stored securely and only shared with parties who need them (lender, title company).
3. **Marketing compliance** — Review your listing descriptions and ad targeting against fair housing rules before publishing.

## When to bring in a professional

- Before you finalize any marketing campaign that targets a specific demographic
- If a transaction has any dispute or unusual complication
- If you're forming a team or brokerage structure and splitting commissions
