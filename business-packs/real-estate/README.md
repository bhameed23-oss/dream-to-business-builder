# Real Estate Business Pack

This pack is designed for real estate businesses, agents, brokerages, and property-focused service companies.

## Business summary

A real estate business sells, buys, rents, manages, or helps people move and invest in property.

## Typical goals

- organize property listings
- qualify leads
- schedule tours
- manage offers and negotiations
- follow up with leads
- automate lead nurture
- coordinate with lenders, inspectors, contractors, and clients
- keep a central database of client and property activity

## Common pain points

- leads go cold
- inquiries are not followed up fast enough
- website leads are not organized
- property data is stored in scattered places
- appointment coordination is manual
- follow-up is inconsistent
- compliance and fair housing concerns need review

## Things to review carefully

This industry commonly involves:
- customer personal information
- property information
- document collection and storage
- financial or mortgage communication
- local legal and fair housing requirements
- lead qualification and follow-up rules

Before launch, check:
- privacy and data handling
- document retention
- consent and communication rules
- anti-discrimination compliance
- secure customer data storage

## Example plan

1. build a simple lead intake flow
2. gather the right information and consent
3. create a CRM or contact system
4. create a lead qualification workflow
5. set up appointment and showing coordination
6. add reminder and follow-up automation
7. review legal and compliance obligations
8. launch with basic security and review processes

## Example automation opportunities

- automatic lead capture from website forms
- SMS and email follow-up templates
- appointment reminders for showings and consultations
- document checklist management
- client status updates
- lead scoring and segmentation
- property match recommendations
- post-appointment follow-up

## Example design direction

- strong trust signals
- clean property portfolio presentation
- clear calls to action
- premium but approachable branding
- mobile-friendly experience
- easy client communication and appointment flow

## Example AI uses

- qualifying leads from inquiry forms
- summarizing client preferences
- helping create follow-up message drafts
- organizing notes from calls and meetings
- generating a property interest summary
- creating a customer experience checklist
