# Real Estate — Tools and Integrations

Real tools, roughly ordered from free/simple to paid/full-featured.

## Lead capture and CRM

- **Google Forms + Google Sheets** — Free way to start capturing leads consistently before investing in a CRM.
- **HubSpot Free CRM** — Free tier, good for organizing contacts and tracking follow-up without cost.
- **Follow Up Boss** — Paid, real-estate-specific CRM built around fast lead response and follow-up sequences. Popular with agents once volume grows past what a free CRM handles well.
- **kvCORE / BoomTown** — Paid, fuller real-estate platforms combining CRM, website, and lead generation — typically for teams or brokerages, not solo agents just starting out.

## Speed-to-lead (the highest-leverage automation in this industry)

- **Follow Up Boss** or similar CRMs above include auto-response and routing — a lead gets an instant text/email reply even if you're not at your phone.
- **Google Voice** — Free business number if you don't already have one separate from your personal cell.

## Transaction coordination

- **Dotloop** or **SkySlope** — Paid, standard tools for managing disclosures, e-signatures, and transaction documents. Many brokerages already provide access to one of these — check before buying your own.

## Scheduling

- **Calendly** — Free/low-cost tier for letting clients book showings or consultations without back-and-forth texting.

## Compliance-relevant note

Whatever CRM or transaction tool you pick, check that it retains required transaction records for as long as your state's real estate commission requires. This varies by state — see compliance-guidance.md in this folder.
