# Legal Contribution Guide

This file is for legal and compliance experts who want to improve the framework.

## What to contribute

- privacy policy templates
- terms of service drafts
- customer data handling guidance
- industry-specific legal checklists
- local compliance guidance
- legal review process improvements
- escalation and professional review triggers

## Best practices

- Write in plain language when possible
- Make the review flow easy to follow
- Add warnings clearly and directly
- Explain that the template is not legal advice
- Provide structure for founders with limited budgets

## Community goal

The goal is not to create fear. The goal is to make legal and compliance review easier, cheaper, and more structured for people who do not know what to do next.

This helps them move smarter and safer.
