# Legal and Compliance Overview

This section is designed to help users think clearly about legal and compliance issues before going live.

## Important reminder

This project helps organize information and steps. It does not replace legal counsel.

## Core legal themes

- customer data handling
- privacy policy needs
- terms of service needs
- retention and deletion policies
- use of third-party tools
- local or industry-specific laws
- regulated information handling
- professional review triggers

## Built-in guardrail language

> This workflow may involve legal or compliance risk. Please review with a lawyer if you are collecting business or customer information, selling regulated goods, processing payments, or working in a regulated area.

## Community contribution

- terms of service templates
- privacy policy templates
- compliance checklists
- simple review frameworks
- legal escalation guidance
