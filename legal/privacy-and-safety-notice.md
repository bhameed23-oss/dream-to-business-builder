# Safety and Legal Notice

This project is designed to help people start and improve businesses while raising useful questions.

## Important legal and risk notices

- This project is educational and operationally helpful, not legal advice.
- It does not replace a lawyer, security professional, accountant, or compliance advisor.
- The use of AI, business automation, customer data, and online systems involves risk.
- People should take time to understand what they are storing and how they are handling it.
- Sensitive or regulated workflows should be reviewed by the proper professional before launch.

## Encouraged safe practice

- use least-privilege access
- keep systems simple at first
- secure customer data
- back up regularly
- use strong authentication
- exercise caution around payments, customer records, and sensitive information
- ask a lawyer before doing anything legally sensitive

## Final reminder

A good system helps people build faster, but no system can remove all business risk. The goal is to reduce avoidable mistakes and help people make smarter, safer decisions.
