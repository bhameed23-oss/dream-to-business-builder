# Community and Open Source Contribution Model

This file outlines how the project can grow through community contribution.

## Contribution areas

- lifecycle phase guidance
- business profile improvements
- compliance and safety review content
- industry-specific example flows
- automation and integration examples
- UI and product improvements
- community support and onboarding documentation

## Contribution principles

- keep the lifecycle structure intact
- prioritize clarity and safety
- avoid vague or unsupported advice
- make beginner-friendly improvements
- ensure high-risk topics include caution and review guidance

## Community story

This project is meant to help a broad range of people who are building or improving businesses, especially those without technical depth, without strong business structure, or without a clear roadmap.

## Why it matters

The project becomes stronger when people from different industries and backgrounds contribute their experience and real-world knowledge.
