# Community Business Pack Contribution Guide

This section is for anyone who wants to contribute a new business pack or example.

## What a business pack can contain

- intake questions specific to the business
- common workflows
- customer journey
- customer touchpoints
- automation opportunities
- compliance notes
- design direction
- launch and review checklist
- example configs or prompts

## Recommended business pack locations

- [business-packs/](./business-packs/)

- construction
- enterprise
- nonprofit

## Contribution best practices

- begin with plain-language context
- explain the business clearly
- include a simple beginner version and a more advanced version
- make it easy to adapt
- add warnings for compliance-sensitive categories
