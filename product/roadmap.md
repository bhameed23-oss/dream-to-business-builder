# Product Roadmap

This file defines the planned evolution of the product from MVP to a fuller platform.

## Phase 1: MVP
- onboarding flow
- intake questions
- business profile creation
- lifecycle phase selection
- dashboard summary
- business health score
- next-step recommendations

## Phase 2: Business system depth
- scaling module
- compliance catch-up module
- automation and systems upgrade module
- deeper tool connector map
- more robust risk scoring

## Phase 3: Workflow integration
- connect local AI tools and automation
- add workflow-level guidance
- provide tool review and permission recommendations
- create more integration patterns for business software

## Phase 4: Expansion and community
- industry-specific templates
- contributed examples from other business types
- stronger public storytelling and education
- community support and contribution model

## Product principle

Every release should reduce confusion and improve business clarity without making the user feel trapped in a rigid process.
