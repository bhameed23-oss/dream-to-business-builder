# Onboarding Questions

This file defines the first-pass onboarding questions for the product.

## Core onboarding questions

1. What type of business do you have?
2. What is your business trying to do right now?
3. What is the biggest issue or pain point right now?
4. What stage are you in?
   - starting
   - operating
   - auditing
   - scaling
   - catching up on compliance
   - upgrading systems
   - preparing for exit
5. What is your main goal in the next 30 to 90 days?
6. What is the biggest risk you are facing right now?
7. What tools or systems are you using today?
8. What would a good outcome look like for this phase?

## Why these questions matter

They are designed to build a useful business profile without overwhelming the user.
