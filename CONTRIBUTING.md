# Contributing Guide

We welcome contributions that help make this framework more powerful, more clear, and more useful.

## Who can contribute

Anyone can contribute, but we especially welcome:

- technical contributors
- designers
- business operators
- automation experts
- security professionals
- legal or compliance professionals
- founders and small business owners
- community members improving the process

## Contribution philosophy

This project is designed to be:
- inclusive
- supportive
- practical
- non-judgmental
- useful to beginners and experts alike

## What kinds of contributions are valuable

- stronger intake questions
- safer business process recommendations
- compliance checklists
- policies and templates
- industry examples
- design system improvements
- automation workflows
- accessibility improvements
- training materials
- community resources

## How to contribute

1. Pick an area of the repo that needs improvement
2. Review the current structure
3. Add or update files in a clear, consistent way
4. Explain why your contribution is useful
5. Include plain-language explanations where possible
6. Keep the project welcoming and beginner-friendly

## Contribution standards

- Use heading and structure consistently
- Prefer clear plain-language explanatory text
- Make things easy for beginners to understand
- Protect users from risky advice by adding warnings where necessary
- Encourage professional review in appropriate cases
- Avoid jargon without explanation

## Code of conduct

This project should remain welcoming, respectful, and educational.

There should be no hostility, gatekeeping, or shaming. People are allowed to be beginners. People are allowed to build a dream. Everyone deserves a path to move forward.
